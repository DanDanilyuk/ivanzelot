In the prime of life
I was raped.
The world's horrors
Fell upon me.

To report it to the organs
Or to stay silent?
…Insult and despair
Must choose:
Let the criminals go on
maiming
Or let them be locked up?

Good people,
Help!
What would be better?
Tell me:
It depends on me: to indulge evil
Or to stop it —
To shove it
Behind bars
Or to grease its palm?
