You are not like all the rest.
You walk your own road.
People are angry at you,
But you are needed by God.

Such as you are.
Ivan, Petro or Judas.
Yes, life beats you,
Tightness from every side.

They say you are going to hell…
Despite countless warnings
You do not drop the cross, you carry it —
God appointed you to remain yourself.
