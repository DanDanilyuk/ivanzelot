I never thought that a divorced woman
I would call to myself.
And she has one more suitor
And the husband will not let go.

Her father gathered us
To his house for counsel,
To settle the problem in peace
And wisely join the family.

The present one jerks about—
Too jealous,
The other has been in love for ages.
And I feel sorry for her —
Let her choose herself.
