The firefighter is ready to leap
At any hour of the day,
Called to the struggle,
So that grief may not come to pass.

The quiet city envies
His pay for doing nothing,
But suddenly it hears the news:
Someone's home has burst into flame.

While they put out the fire
He will save several lives.
They will carry the hero in their arms
And forget him in a few days.
