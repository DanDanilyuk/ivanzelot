It wounded the soldier
Alone in the trench.
Curious, how too many
Are the saved in his work?

What sort of shrapnel
Pierced him through?
The obituary can wait,
Let him still live.

To save more lives,
He has to ignore his own.
The warrior is ready for self-giving
For the freedom
Of the people,
For a better fate.
