In the chase for money they are right here, the competitors.
A beloved craft — friends and fruits,
Not percentages of excessive profits,
Not spies and not counterparties.
Enjoy, create, and live.

Not scraps of paper, not metal, and not stone,
But precious treasures of the heart
Resistant to the lures of glory —
Her kisses are dangerous.

Greed in finances
And swollen pride —
Attributes of lordship,
But wait for trouble.
