Constant comfort spoils.
Come, problem, refresh my gratitude.
Struggle with you completes well-being,
Perfect me and run to others.

You are called to strengthen
The weak and the fragile
Without a pacifier in the cradle
And with porridge in the bowl,
By the blackboard at school
And on the football pitch.
The exercise is written
And the book is read —
The hard has become EASY and accessible.

It's a sin to complain about challenges-hardships,
About the true invisible teachers.
They add patience and courage,
They form a hero, as I wanted.
