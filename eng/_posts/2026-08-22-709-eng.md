War broke the families,
Crippled the fates,
You cannot drive the predator out of a human,
He constantly craves blood.

Having borne insane losses,
He dreams of righteous revenge.
The offender must be repaid —
So holiness forgets about chastity.

Not having agreed with the enemy whose is whose,
Again he goes to sort it out:
Defends,
Or takes back what is his.
