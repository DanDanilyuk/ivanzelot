Nobody, somebody, some, each, all

Nobody of nobody to nobody by nobody
Nothing of nothing to nothing by nothing
Nowhere no-how by no kind
From nowhere to nowhere never —
There are no teachers, nor pupils, nor school.

Somebody of somebody to somebody by somebody
Something of something for something by something
Somewhere somehow by some kind
From somewhere sometime toward somewhere —
It became a phenomenon. Learn…
Some of some to some by some
Somewhat of somewhat to somewhat by somewhat
By some all around, here and there
Elsewhere some-way sometimes —
We'll accept or we'll deny.

Each of each to each by each
Becomes submissive or hostile…
All by all to all for all
(Both us and them)
In every way of every kind everywhere
All over always from every side
Everything of everything of all —
However sin resisted,
God's goal is attained.
As the Covenant declared,
The world filled with blessings.
