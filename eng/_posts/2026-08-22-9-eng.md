I still can't fathom
how one can
love Ukraine —
"With such deep feelings"
and drown her in Russian.

What kind of Ukrainian
has a katsap's tongue?
Who would replace the mother
with father and brother?

Though it is unseemly,
he'll say "what's the difference"
about the foreign, even the hostile,
and lie down under the oppressor?
