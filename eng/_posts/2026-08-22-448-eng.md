QR, barcodes, words, programs,
Rises, dips, ones, zeroes,
To meet in tastes,
To coincide in thoughts.
To appear, to vanish from the surface of the earth.

With one's presence to make someone happy,
Not to become an obstacle on the highway,
To pour a heap of grievances on a scoundrel,
To settle onto a horse, and it will carry you.

Crawl up and jump back,
Flee and come.
Release and support —

Only agree it with the code.
