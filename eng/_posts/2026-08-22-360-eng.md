A lamb, a ewe, and a ram —
As if one family.
The meek, the dull, and the tyrant —
And how is a human different?

Dull and meek — a simpleton.
All are bad when set on evil.
The stubborn — a hero and a leader,
Wonderful, if set on good.

Everyone fears God,
For they sin, for conscience judges.
If only to reach such a level
As to love Him instead —
Meekly, dully, or with the horn.
