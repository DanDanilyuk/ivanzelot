Over the fight in the Donetsk ring
Billions of angels watch.
It does not interest them when in the first minute
And in the first round

Goliath knocks down David.
This is not a show.
Let disgust grow first
Toward the aggressor who came with a sword.

Let the armies writhe,
Let fears pass among the estates,
Let each one's intentions be rethought,
Before the stone flies.
