Perfect love,
Described in novels,
Tried by time,
Knew no betrayals
(Or hid them well).

She accepted discords
As a blessing,
Drank her fill of freedom
And of delight,
Tempered her patience.

She passed by temptations,
Overcame obstacles
Perfect love,
…For she loved life.
