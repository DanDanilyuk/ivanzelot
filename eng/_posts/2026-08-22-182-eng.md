The chemical cause of the illness
They will find
In the laboratory.
They will give pills, mixtures,
Or bring you back to life
As an outpatient.

Medications replenish the lack in the body —
I take them and go on sinning.
Why do I rush to burn?
Or maybe take another way toward the goal?

The overloaded shelves
Of pharmaceutical poison
Will be replaced by mended habits,
So the hospital may be forgotten.
