Stars are falling —
Hold out your cap.
If you yourself break loose —
They'll catch you.
Fireflies from on high
As if placing a period
On times with no beginning —
Immortality calls.

Eternity writes —
Read,
Attend carefully
To her lessons.
Spacious or cramped —
Gather,
So that attainable
Peace may prove.

To earth
A star hurried,
Dissolved and went out —
You won't find it.
But in the dark dome
It punched a hole.
In due time
Through it
You'll fall into the sky.
