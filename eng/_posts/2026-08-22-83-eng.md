Which came first,
The chicken or the egg?
A rhetorical question.
Sarmat, or Pershing?
This, or that? —
Lying to orcs is the custom.

Of course “theirs is better,
It is more advanced.”
“For it is harder on the enemy,
For theirs strikes harder.”

“They did not even attack
And the truth is theirs,”
“They were only defending”
What is foreign, already their own.
