The flags grow fewer —
They've grown tired.
Indifference is the first
To flee from the groans.

We're sick of depression,
We want victories.
Aggression is forgotten —
Enough of the alarms.

The neighbor's house
Is already burning down.
And I have to plow —
The field is waiting.
