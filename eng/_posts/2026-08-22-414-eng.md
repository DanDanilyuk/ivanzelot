Once you've begun, you won't stop —
The format will change.
You won't shut yourself in
Without achievements and losses.

Opposites — in unity.
Acknowledge and believe.
From zero to boundlessness,
From a spark to the stars.

After an addition, another addition,
After completion — continuation too.
Deep in the germ sits the beginning,
And the space for growth has no bounds.
