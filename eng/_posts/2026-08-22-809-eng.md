The muse— not a whore, and a whore — not a muse,
Though they are often confused.
When they listen with a pure heart,
The muse fortifies the marriage —
Inspires the wife and captivates the husband,

There is no betrayal, ready for sacrifice
Are both the muse and her happy chosen ones —
A persistent, stubborn character
They demonstrate, as if in a dance.

They avidly overcome
All obstacles,
Ignoring earthly rewards,
They seek ideals beyond the clouds.
