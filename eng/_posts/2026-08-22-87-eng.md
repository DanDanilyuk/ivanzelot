They gain their experience
By a rich sacrifice.
But they mark as guilty
The benefactors, in the end.

The indifferent
As if they never were —
Made peace with the enemy.
And the friend is no longer a friend —
They quarreled with him.

Whoever gave sincerely,
From him more was asked.
And whoever slowed the pace —
On him they poured their rage.
