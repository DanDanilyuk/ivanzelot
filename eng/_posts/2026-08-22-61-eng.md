Energy pours out
Into the chalice of eternity,
From each is expected
To shed the worse.

Even in evil
They seek the better.
If not for others, then for themselves
A greater worth.

Eternity receives
From the living and the dead,
From the whole and the worn-down.
Whoever it receives from
Draws from it as well.
