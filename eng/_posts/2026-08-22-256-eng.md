To die attacking
Or defending —
The choice is clear: hell or paradise.
Victorious Cain,
Gaining the world,
Lose yourself.

A leader or a lackey,
A master or a slave,
Of two harbors only
Discern at least,

Which is profitable for you
Be it for a year
Or for an age…
To become banished from Life
A man decides…
