They say to investigate is to stop living.
For a participant to become an observer,
To step out of the test tube, to look into it…
Is that not to find oneself nearby,
It turns out, with the Creator?

To refuse to remain a game,
For a pawn, not quite a queen,
Having dodged attack and battle,
To sit at the board as a player.

What complaints? FLOORS
Each one chooses for himself:
Physically or in thought
They play him, or he plays.
And by playing,
Not abandoning the game,
Does he not a greater level
Of life achieve?
