The intimacy of conception
And the intimacy of departure.
Without haste and without delay —
However you try to sway nature.

I seem to be planning,
I choose the moment,
I think, I control,
And I fall into the noose.

Experienced fate
In service of the IDEA
Is meant to catch me —
I will not escape her.
