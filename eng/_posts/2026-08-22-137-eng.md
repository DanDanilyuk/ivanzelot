You flee or you catch up,
You find or you have lost,
You are shackled or you fly,
You meet or you never met.

Happy or unhappy,
Afraid or a hero,
Free or subject,
U-ha-ha-ha or o-yoy-YOY.

In church or at work,
In a strange city or in your own village,
Licentious or in chastity —
In a dream…
