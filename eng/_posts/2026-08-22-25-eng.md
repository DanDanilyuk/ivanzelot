You see dimly.
You never know
Where you'll lose,
And where you'll meet.

You met, it seemed, a friend —
He became the enemy.
Outrage seized you —
God gave a little luck.

Conquered by fate
Ready for anything.
He'll rejoice at freedom,
But he'll bear slavery too.
