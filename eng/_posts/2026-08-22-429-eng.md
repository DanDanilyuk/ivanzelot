Whoever speaks Russian
Becomes a "russkiy."
The orc doesn't occupy — he "liberates,"
Takes back "what's his."

Where the strangers are, where ours are —
It's simple to tell:
The language of "rasha" —
And that is enough.

Switch over to it
And call down trouble.
Soon you'll hear from the trench:
"Asvabazhdat' idu!"
