If only one could scoop from the night,
Cool the day —
Anomalous heat rages…
Nature does not want
The harmful inspirations
With which man destroys.

It hints and asks:
"Children of the planet!
Come to your senses!
Calm down! Enough!
Dismantle the rockets!
Make peace!

To be enemies
And compete,
To plunder
And seek excessive good
Cease!
Cease
Crossing the bounds!
Start becoming brothers,
Do not kill,
Live,
Climb down
From the tower of Babel."
