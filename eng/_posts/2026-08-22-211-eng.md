Knowledge leads to a result.
The GOOD to blessings, the evil to sufferings.
It is easy to die in happiness and love,
Having learned to accept even death
As the fulfillment of cherished desires.

Hell in eternity, horror on earth
For the SOWER of HATRED.
On God's scale
Below and on high
Are stripped of certainty
The seekers of intrigues.

The convinced righteous one
Is on the straight paths,
In treasures unstolen,
Meant for God,
Though they look like his own.
