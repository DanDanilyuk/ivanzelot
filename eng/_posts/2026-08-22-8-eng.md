What does he eat with his ears?
I would fall ill from that.
Anger would tear me apart
from contempt for the evil.

An allergy has come
to the propagandists.
Hope remains —
there will be no rashists.

I have no health left
to drink the slop.
If only the war would die out,
I want to live.
