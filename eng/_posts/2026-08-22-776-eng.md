As thread follows the needle, thought follows the idea
Inspires or saddens,
Puts the will into the stance "attention"
Or forces it to flee.

It drinks from the source considered faith,
Grows into words and deeds,
Charitable or extremely lustful
It lays a path to glory.

The operator-thought is a gain of society.
A droplet for a city,
A country, the earth,
Among the stars a tiny spark —
The universe values it highly.
