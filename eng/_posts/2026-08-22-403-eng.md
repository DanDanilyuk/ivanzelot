To meet with dignity the one born into the world
And to see them off with dignity —
The responsibility of each
And of society,
To carry the spark of life together,
Not to extinguish God's act.

Your flash, brother,
And sister's firework
In starry glories
The worlds see.

In vain you grieve
Over your own worthlessness.
Believe and you will feel
Certainty in eternity.
