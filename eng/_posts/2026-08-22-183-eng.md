We stood on the veranda
To watch
The world's collapse,
How, as if on command,
They will start destroying
Us and you.

An interesting show —
We have waited a long time,
Though we delayed it,
But at last again
They have fetched out the war.

"Surely not the last?" —
Adrenaline asks.
This is the question
Of many generations.
