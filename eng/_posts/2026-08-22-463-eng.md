Who is Abel?
A myth, a legend, inventions.
In every contest the stronger wins.
He survived — so he is Cain,
He killed the holy one.
Thus the worse one triumphs.

For the murderer — glory, compliments and honors,
Pyramids, tombs and mausoleums.
To destroy a brother,
To take the crown,
To turn beast over it —
Turns out to be a mark of valor.

But the true crown is for those who suffered,
For those who did not steal,
Did not take,
But paid.
Who with love stopped
The rabid,
Who rose from the dead
And came alive for the Kingdom.
