One may envy
The old emigration,
Advise others
To dissident actions.

But the tragedy of resettlement
Is not only in nostalgia.
It is in the transfer
Into alien conditions.

Do not judge the hunted
Who preserved his life,
From the much-suffering
He ran too far.
