A chamomile and a butterfly are meant to be friends —
Nature is in harmony.
And suddenly — liquid soap —
Bubbles playfully
Inflate the air.

Colors in iridescence
Will scatter all around.
For less than minutes
They will live above the field.

Soap, chamomile, and butterfly
Are happy to serve even briefly
For merry moments
In a short wonder.
