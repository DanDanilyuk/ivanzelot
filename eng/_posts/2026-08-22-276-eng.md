The charms of allure,
Attributes of love —
Peignoirs and accessories
For fastening and taking off.

To the peak of achievements
The last obstacle.
For putting on and stripping off
Their nature.

Carriers of mysteriousness,
A proposition of oneself,
Erotica in industry —
For the buyer to catch fire,
For the seller to grow rich.
