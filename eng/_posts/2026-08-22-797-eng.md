Like the other prophets,
Christ did not create,
He explained
The deep Design
Of Life itself,
By the power of Words
Of the Holes of Nonbeing
The disturbed peace,

A flash of Love,
Light and Warmth,
In which burn
Death and untruth.
And scientific thoughts
At first for evil,
As protest, arise,
To glorify the Author tomorrow.

Crucified, he died,
Revealing the Mystery by Resurrection,
Whoever believed knows now
To what the Most High calls.
