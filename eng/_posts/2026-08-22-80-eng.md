Life — a scale,
Effort — a rudder.
Truth — fullness,
Absence — zero.

Motion — while you go,
You snag — you're torn.
You seek — you live.
You reached it — meaning you died.

The perfect — unreachable.
Good— boundless.
Evil will prove it.
Learn,
But carefully!!!
Don't stumble.
