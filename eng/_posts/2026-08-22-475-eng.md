One day far from the city
In the middle of a field
Suddenly
Someone burst.

Sworn brothers were loafing nearby,
A watermelon suddenly split.
Ripe, dark-red, juicy.
Kherson.

No matter that the farmer was gathering hops.
Brothers and sisters marveled,
And there was no one with a knife.

The watermelon proclaimed Glory to Life !
