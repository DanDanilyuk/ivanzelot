Natural selection is merciless to the feeble,
A mistake is threatened with leaving the TRACK,
Success waits for the zealous,
The ardent one builds a nest for the family.

A leader comes to power with an idea,
With the Quran, with the New Testament of Christ,
He assures of equality, of brotherhood,
But to assert himself is his goal.

Under the mask of one inclined toward truth
With a strong fist
He demonstrates around him the right of the strong,
Saying, the crucified one was a weakling.
