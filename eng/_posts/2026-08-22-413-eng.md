Wounds will heal,
Hopes will be fulfilled —
In reverse perspective:
The later, the more.
With delay, higher quality.
Patience is not weakness.
They hurry toward worse,
And the prudent are often happy.

I know what is wanted,
And I'd like it already.
Success asks for itself,
Presses and drives.

Don't hurry much, but don't miss it either.
The hunter is the one who shoots true:
A fraction of a second — aimed, hit.
But he waits long for that moment.
