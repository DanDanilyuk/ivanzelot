Were it not for a new day,
Were it not for the resurrection,
There would be fewer songs,
Hope of salvation.

If Christ had not died
To overcome death,
What would we live by now?
What would our cares be?

We would fear the end
That reduces all to nothing,
We would not know the future
That passes into eternity.
