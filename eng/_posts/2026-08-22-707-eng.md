rfia is "mighty" here too —
It changes Ukrainian spelling.
In pu's surname — a part is missing,
Like rust or chaff, sediment or oxide.

Huge letters become small,
Like puffed-up imperialness,
After the bites of the moscow-viper
The poison must be neutralized.

So linguistic immunity is at work,
To save the host is its mission.
The stronger the thought IN Ukraine,
The faster raissia will break its teeth.
