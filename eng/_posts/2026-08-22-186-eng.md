Ivan ZELot lives in me.
I carry him constantly in myself.
Sometimes he speaks inspired words,
When I ask, or even when I don't.

I love to listen to him —
A wise advisor.
He offers to stir thought awake,
Weeds the nursery of ideas.

Thank you, Ivan,
That you care so.
Especially at night,
When I sleep, and You – labor...
