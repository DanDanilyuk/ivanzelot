It's time to move the capital,
For Kyiv has suddenly vanished somewhere.
Ukrainian no longer suits it —
Now the tongue reigns here.

Vinnytsia, Khmelnytskyi rejoice —
The tongue has narrowed the country,
They will take over Ukraine.
For a rapist has entered Kyiv.

The Kyiv Moscow gubernia —
Rocket and bayonet did their work.
It marches toward Lviv from Azovsk,
Ukrainskiy russkiy yazyk.
