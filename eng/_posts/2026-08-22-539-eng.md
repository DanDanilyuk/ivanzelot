Come what may, waking in the morning,
Lady Panic will not stage a scandal.
What was continued yesterday in hourly labor
Will soothe the strain, stave off collapse.

Nerves will hold, emotions stay healthy,
A festive mood, a balanced pressure.
With vigor, in a new encouragement
The diligent will show uncommon skill.

We hear it in the news, we read it in the paper —
The planet is short of peace.
She has found herself in alarm,…
Because someone cannot overcome their own.
