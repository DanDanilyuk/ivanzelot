I am a small person,
But I long for something great,
So that Ukraine might LIVE
Without a wild "brother."

With his "tongue"
And mania of "greatness,"
With the Führer, Puilo —
A gopnik without "propriety."

I grow more convinced —
We will sweep away the obstacles.
I cannot manage it alone,
But there are millions of us.
