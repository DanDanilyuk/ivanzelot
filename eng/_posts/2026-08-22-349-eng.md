Imagined jealousy breaks fates.
Absence of trust destroys relationships.
Yesterday's heroes become nobodies —
And no facts are needed, a thought is enough.

You take revenge on the dearest, and you suffer yourself,
You stir up feelings and emotions in yourself.
Yesterday you loved, and today it is war.
Instead of embracing, you grumble and shove.

You will not kiss and will not caress.
He didn't deserve it; someone else should have cared for you.
As if you love him so much,
But in truth — you rake it all toward yourself.
