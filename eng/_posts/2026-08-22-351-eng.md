I wait.
And you, hiding beyond the window, watch
Whether I have
Need of you.
It dawns.
And how much you will unsettle me
Is of great interest to the sky.

Will you remain unnoticed, or will they call you into the house?
Will they drive you off, or meet you with embraces.
Will you dictate words — will they write them down?
Will they take the advice or reject it?
Will they scorn it, or keep it in the heart?

I will notice, I will call,
I will EMBRACE and write it down.
Both slogan and parable
I will leave to descendants.
