And why not break oneself?
A change of poles will spin the opposite way.
Instead of envying and lying
To change the attitude —
And it will let go.

In loss we will recognize the gain,
In scoundrels — those who tomorrow repent.
From the hollows we will head toward the exit,
We will cheer those barked at by the rabble.

We will stop enduring rudeness
And walking the paths of untruth —
Beggars will turn into lords,
In anger we will learn to love.
