Weigh yourself at evening and at morning.
The soul's weight is the difference.
In you, her chosen one,
She cannot sit still.

When you sleep,
She leaves your body.
She flies somewhere, and you lie there,
So the body can rest.

She is cramped with you,
Boundlessness is her element.
To finish the mission quickly,
To return to eternity — is her dream.
