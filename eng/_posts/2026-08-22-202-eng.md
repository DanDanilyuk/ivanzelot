Speak, SOUL, lie down in lines
That are rhymed,
Seal yourself with thoughts
That are ordered.

The anxiety of unclarity
Change with PEACE,
The fog of indifference
Dispel with the word.

Falling and stumbling,
They attain learning.
Composing themselves in verse,
They spare their legs and arms.
