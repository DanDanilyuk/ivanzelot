When they leave you alone, there are no challenges —
You atrophy.
Life is motion.
War comes —
You wake and you build yourself,
You defend yourself, the spirit grows stronger.

It is hard. You pass through I-cannot.
No one envies you, they even turn away.
They do not expect
That you will win,
Confidence is needed— without it they break.

Another's and one's own,
Especially one's own.
So let it not go out!
In faith — there is life!
