Victories intoxicate, but they also relax you.
You become inattentive and overconfident.
Your enemies know this.
That is why they DIG under you,
So you end up defiled.

So that you fall
And do not rise,
Lose the battle
And become a captive,
Tremble and be afraid.

STEALING
And taking
Closeness from a thief —
The law of nature.
Victories,
Trophies and awards —
That is the humiliation of the weak.
