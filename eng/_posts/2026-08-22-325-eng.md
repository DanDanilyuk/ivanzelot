For some the weekend arrived,
Others were SWEPT to rallies.
Each has his own trade —
It happened.

For someone a SHOT was planned,
For the bodyguards, a chance to show themselves,
The candidate for leader was maimed,
And someone else was left dead.

You think they wounded the speaker?
They shoved America.
Where will we end up, you and I?
It is all still AHEAD.
