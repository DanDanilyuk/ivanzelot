To answer well
Need not be in verse.
In prose a message
Will serve no worse.

At least one word
You can say.
And that's it — done.
Nothing to add.

Thank you. Dziękuję.
Pięknie. Wonderfully.
Congratulations. Gratuluję.
Nobly. Honorowo.
