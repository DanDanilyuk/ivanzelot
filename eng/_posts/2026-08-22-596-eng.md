To see the soul —
That is what my correspondence is for.
I have read yours and I must
Show mine up close.

Someone will understand the music,
Someone needs words,
Someone focused on a button —
Beauty attracts in it.

Even when they ignore,
It has not grown worse, but easier:
They paint a self-portrait of thought —
I LOOK into the mirror.
