A golden iceberg with a peak of faith —
In fact a mountain
That with precious stones
GREW down to the bottom.

Not a floating fragment,
But a protruding foundation.
A linguoseismologist
Would simply say: the Word.

Confidence and anxiety
Of abysses and mountains —
Your victory.
Hope and believe!
