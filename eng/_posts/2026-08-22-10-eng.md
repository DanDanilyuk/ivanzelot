Inhabit the earth,
change your fathers.
Shine where it is dark,
where there are no flags.

I will go before you,
I will pave the ways for you,
with your heels
I will crush the serpent.

I could do it Myself.
But in Glory
I give it to you too.
You and I —
we go as a pair.
