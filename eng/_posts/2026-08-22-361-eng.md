Gnaw at each other over faith,
Argue about language,
About insincere “Gayropa,”
About military impotence.

What kind of people are you,
What nation is that?
You will be relieved of worries
Only by the Kremlin's operation.

Moscow's liberators
With tanks and automatic rifles...
With them you are not inhabitants,
Except perhaps behind bars.
