To neutralize betrayal with betrayal
Both he and she decided —
They tried to save love,
Sinking lower than the bottom.

For lovers — success, amusement,
The intoxicating wine of temptation,
And in the result — a divorced couple,
And the fortune they built brought to nothing.

He called them into a phantom reality,
BROUGHT it into both their minds,
So they would GO past the extreme,
Roses and poison — Phantom.
