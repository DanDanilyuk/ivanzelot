Labels and wrappers —
An explanation of the name.
The thing is known at once,
Its signs recognized:
Bitter or sweet,
Hay in a sack, or straw?

What is named — is numbered among knowledge,
It lies in the archive, written down.
The beloved are sung in songs.
And how to understand without a name?

"Love," "suffering,"
"Skirts," "spreaders,"
"Marichka," "desire" —
Word-wrappers.
