There are not enough volunteers to defend.
Drive them to the front by force?
Obviously yes,
When there is no one left to fight.

From the conscious — blocking detachments,
Onto minefields — the unreliable.
Such cruel  laws.
That is what wars are for.

Sorry to lose people?
Then they improve the weapon  —
At the cost of enemy deaths
They will save you and me.
