Though the skeleton is already without skin,
Without innards and without flesh,
They hold in faith
To sky-high goals:
The skeleton is our foundation.

He is the soul's carrier,
A reliable frame,
Having brought to the fulfillment of dreams,
Willing to lead to the next.

To thank the skeleton
With muscles or without
The poet is itching,
Before the impulse vanishes.
