Lucky the last one,
Who jumped on the tram's running board.
To him, the one at the edge,
First to get off.

He didn't freeze long at the stop,
Maybe even rode sitting,
At the exit he skipped the queues,
Without a spare “excuse me.”

Lucky who makes it everywhere,
Dodging the traffics.
Fate helps him,
Teaches him to read the schedules.
