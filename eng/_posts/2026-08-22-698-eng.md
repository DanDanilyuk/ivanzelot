First the microbe
Attacked the bacterium,
It came to attempts
To set forehead to forehead
Nuclear artillery.

The cause of Armageddon
Seems to be artillery.
But guilty of it all
Are the microbe and the bacterium.

A thought accepted
By a plan of action.
Developed into a global conflict —
Rejoice if you want,
Pity if you want.
