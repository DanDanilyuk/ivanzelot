&@
Real roads are paved with mirages,
Dreams call us to bring the universe to life.
At first glance the power is lacking,
Then suddenly — civilization at the crossroads.

You have not even thought, and someone has already done it,
Imagination also competes.
Others have passed where you have not walked,
The bio-anthill seethes and swirls.

Someone was trampled, someone tramples himself,
What the scramble is for, they never fully said.
Make way for new mirages!
For the affirmation of complete Glory.
