Friends swore loyalty,
But, having broken etiquette,
In mutual tension
They reached for pistols.

Access to many capacities —
Etiquette.
The revealing of identities in contradictions —
A priority in relations.

We will keep our friends,
We will save our cartridges,
When of murderous illusions
We remove the obstacles.
