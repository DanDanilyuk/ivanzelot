No pity for outsiders,
Until you find yourself out.
It turns out nature
Draws closed graphs.

It lifts you on a wave of success,
If only one could scoop still more stars...
He does not lose his smile
Who takes a step into the valley.

Having accepted the law of gravity,
He calmly crosses out the days,
Gives away shares for free,
For a star is waiting at the bottom.
