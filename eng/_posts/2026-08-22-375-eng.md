Not in empty words do they give themselves away,
But in the literal sense,
When the force goes outward,
Leaving the body neutral.

Breathless, entirely motionless,
Needed by no one,
Forgotten or very well known.
But the energy steps into another home.

Into the air, the water, the specks of dust around.
In a house far more spacious
The Word will continue to live,
In a more perfect, wider form.
