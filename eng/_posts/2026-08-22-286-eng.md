For the turtle to burrow into silt,
For the eagle to rise above the clouds,
They fear sharks in the seas,
Flocks graze in peace.

But a human,
Pilot, miner, or shepherd,
A submerged submarine,
Cannot quiet their spirit,
To reign — that is the main reason.

Beyond the Universe's threshold,
What is going on there?
Until they become God,
They will not rest.
