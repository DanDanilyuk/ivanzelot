A special status — a wife.
The neighbor has his, and I have mine.
They also say: a half,
With her we are a family together.

Between us it is not merely friendship,
But a domestic forge of patience,
A loving mutual service
To strengthen the family roots.

A miracle of nature,
Two particles — a WHOle.
Their union builds a staircase,
To carry the living into eternity.
