I already see:
To say a museum will buy it
Is not enough.
The painting speaks to all people,
Universally.

Dignity in the face,
A soul in the chest.
It breathes with lightness
Of byssus, not brocade.

Yellow-and-blue
Inspiration has come.
"The Unshakable Ukrainian"
Is the beauty's name.
