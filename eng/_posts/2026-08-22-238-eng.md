The careless thing left orbit,
I broke the system of comfort,
I will begin to truly value
The dearest thing I had to hold.

It did not leave to the end,
Though the traitor was not someone else, but I, —
It remained a symbol of hope,
A repentance for the return.

Possibility waits patiently,
I correct the mistake at once.
Persistence promises
The fulfillment of old dreams.
