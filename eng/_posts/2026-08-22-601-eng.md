Matches and a razor in the cradle of mankind
Tossed in on purpose, or by chance?
Intellect and curiosity fight for experience,
So that a NEW Word might open.

Pain, Fire, Blood and Conflagration.
Cruel lessons, but there is no other way.
Atom, Electricity, the Internet-Network,
Power's hungry maniac.

The most dangerous forms the best —
Let us learn, brothers and sisters,
Not to burn ourselves, and with ourselves the rest.
We hope Daddy is keeping us in check?
