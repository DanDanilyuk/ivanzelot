A ground of blood,
Center of the universe,
A dispute in the word,
Not even a whisper left.

An echo of history,
Distorted by statistics,
A trampled shoot,
Murdered in the bud.

Born for eternity,
Deprived of life.
Not without the Creator's cunning
Sent into eternity.
(Abel)
