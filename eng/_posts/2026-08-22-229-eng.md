SOUND covers space,
HABIT — covered the process with itself.
The eye asks its LID to close,
WAXed — covered, smeared.

A VECTOR to cover distance,
WALK'd — covered, walked,
AGE — a covering of years.
To coVER — to pass, to skip,
The MOUTH let through —
Meaning it ate.

sCREWed — skipped a screw,
SPENT — let through what he had,
WIRE they pass through, like a probe.
Drew with a KRETKA
(skipped).
What a KRETKA is — some knew, some didn't.
