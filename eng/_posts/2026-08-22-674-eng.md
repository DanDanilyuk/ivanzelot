Controversy is a condition for the search for truth,
Unipolarity —
A falling into extremity.
Light and vibe are granted by sparks,
Their nature — the presence of contradictions.

A short peace — the reward for wars,
Sweet sleep — the laborer's,
The longer the parting, the stronger the embrace,
For calendars there are no stops.

And here a thought has woken in bed —
A new day demands motion,
They have woken us to feats,
Have lit the sun again,
Our nearest star.
Time to get up — the struggle awaits.
