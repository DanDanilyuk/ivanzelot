No one heals like that,
As the Physician above physicians.
And no one will provide,
As the Rich One above the rich.

And there is no better teacher
Than the Teacher above teachers.
He is everywhere,
Inside and among us.

By Him we breathe and live,
If only we would keep company with Him,
If only we would bow to Him —
But not all of us recognize Him.
