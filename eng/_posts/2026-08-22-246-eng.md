I pollinate with the wind,
I leave my problems to nature.
I go after the light,
Theorems lead me.

I develop and complete,
I help, without interfering.
I pull and I push,
I fan, without blowing out,

If I have the chance —
I will fix it.
If I cannot, but I know —
I will say it.
