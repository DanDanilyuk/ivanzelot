People flee from grief,
They avoid misfortune —
They seek shelter.
From mountain to mountain
They crawl and they fly,
From sea to sea.

Among a foreign people,
They dress themselves in feathers —
To fleece later
The sponsors they try.

Be Abel —
Help your brother.
With axe and stone —
Your own executioner.
