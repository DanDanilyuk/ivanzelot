White and black,
Manifest and nonexistent,
Sad and beneficent,
Dead and seething.

Mixed by relativity,
Filled with shades.
Where needed, hurried,
And somewhere accelerated.

Pinched between the poles
DEPENDENCE ON THE ORBITS,
Though bounded by edges,
Doomed to boundlessness.
