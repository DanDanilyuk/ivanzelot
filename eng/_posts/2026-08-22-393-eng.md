A fierce position. Radicalism.
Cemeteries filled with crosses.
Fascism or communism,
Trumpism or Putinism —
Corridors of fates prepared for peoples.

You complain the predecessors chose wrong —
They brought grief, suffering, and trouble?
You fence yourself off, you find intermediaries —
Let them vote, and I won't go.

Non-participation is also radicalism.
From it, still more graves.
A considered choice and wise action
Will steer the gathered strength toward the good.
