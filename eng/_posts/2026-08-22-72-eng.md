With taxes
They rob the population,
To fill with fortunes
"The sky-dwellers' palaces."

That is why maidans
Are in Zaporizhian genes,
To tear the shackles
In corruption schemes.

The tax office is needed
For the good of the people.
Uncontrolled — it is false,
It inflicts harm.
