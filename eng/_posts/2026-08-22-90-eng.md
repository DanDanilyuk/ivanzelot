A strong prayer
Of a pickpocket
And of one ready to die
In bloody battles.

A prayer is heard
Of the hilled-up and the tied,
Not spoken aloud
And not poured out in thought.

So why are you ashamed
Of your own,
In happiness, in sorrow?
A tomato prays.
Is it not time for you too?
