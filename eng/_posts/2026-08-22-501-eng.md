I look at the mountains,
Someone is interested in the forest,
Someone talks about the war,
Someone's nose is stuffed.

Everyone has their own problems
And everyone has their own ache.
That is why on different themes
It boils in our thoughts.

You tell best about your own,
Another's soul is fog.
You counsel yourself in verse,
And let each one search in themselves.
