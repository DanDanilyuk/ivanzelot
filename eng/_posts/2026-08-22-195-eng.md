If people did not repay the debt,
And conservation of energy is an unshakable law,
Why consider that the money is gone,
That you only sacrifice, and someone is insatiable?

Karma watches carefully
How the sincere one gives,
Does not take away.
It heals misfortune,
And covers the outlays.

They did not return it?
You invested.
They forgot?
In heaven they saw and heard,
God compensated.
