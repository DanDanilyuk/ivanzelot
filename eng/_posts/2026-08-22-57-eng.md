What have you done, Dmytro?
You swapped out a person.
You changed their way of thinking,
Peace abandoned me.

You harnessed me to the phone,
I only press the buttons.
To what manner of exploits
Do you call me without meaning to.

I know, I know, it is not you.
You only prayed.
We go toward one goal,
So that God may be praised.
