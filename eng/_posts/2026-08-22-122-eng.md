That one fell, broke his spine,
That one died of cancer,
That one a knuckleduster killed in the zone,
SHE did not wake in the morning…

This is how we judge others,
As if they lived to die.
From the bad to the worst,
We ascribe everything to death…

… He raised five children,
Wrote four books,
Defended seven people,
Like no one else, she did haircuts…
