The saints will demarcate the borders,
Mark them with their bodies.
Blocking detachments from behind
Will reconsecrate them with fugitives.

The first saints — patriots.
After them — saints for salaries.
Then the sainted idiots.
And further one must search.

The homeless, vagrants, addicts —
Consecrate the border with whatever comes
Against the szlachta, the moskals, or the Ottomans,
So the sinful elites survive.
