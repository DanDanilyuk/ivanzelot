Valentine's Day
From a man's point of view (-:

My other half,
Fullness of life's meaning,
You aim me swiftly at the goal,
And without you the goal isn't the same.

You — the axe to the handle,
The teeth to the key.
Ink for writing,
Soul for a lonely body.

Capability for the weakened,
Fuel for the engine.
You drive away loneliness,
That's why you are so dear.
