The sorrowful together with the downcast
Rose up against the grief of the world
To remind those deceived by Satan
Of the essence of God's Covenant.

They are called sectarians,
Confused with swindlers,
But they, as Atlases,
Wage war on sins.

Destitute sufferers,
Thrown into dumpsters,
Mocked and unconquered
Wrapped in sackcloth,
In camel's garments,
To bring to repentance, to convert,
They call those who still care
To meet the Messiah.
