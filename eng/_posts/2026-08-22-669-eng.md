A filled-in well gives no water,
Refusal of faith paralyzes,
Absence of morality rebels,
The unwise apostate beats himself,
Does not take and does not hear sound advice.

A limited ego without intellect
Inflicts plenty of ruin,
Posing a danger
To neighboring countries as well.

In the status of the evil and the wretched
After lunch he will come to you too,
Having freed himself of his own good,
He will free others' good from them as well.
