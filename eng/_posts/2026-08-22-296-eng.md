Mobilization.
The nation is tired.
Motivation is weak.
They came for me too.
On the battlefield
Heroes perish
For you and me.

For us cowards,
Fugitives and draft dodgers,
Friends die
At the occupiers' hands.

One has to choose
Alone with one's consciousness:
To give a bribe,
Or follow one's conscience?
