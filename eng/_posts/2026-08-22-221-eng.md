No time for romance,
Though the romantics ask,
Give us wrappers at least,
Since they bring no candy.

Better still — compassion,
At least a crumb of humanity.
Now we are not merely trash,
But the biowaste of alienation.

Pumped full of HATRED —
Outsiders and our own.
We are lost to the universe,
Still alive for a minute.
