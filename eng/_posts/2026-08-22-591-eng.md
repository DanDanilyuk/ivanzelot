The old teach the young,
Leaving experience to descendants —
The empty are awaited above,
To be handed to higher riddles.

Children correct the parents —
The missed catches up with the seniors.
And from small teachers
The wise sometimes hear it voiced.

Student and professor on both sides,
A mixed category.
They give and take lessons,
The department and the lecture hall.
