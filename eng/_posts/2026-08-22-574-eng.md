I ask for advice to choose perfectly,
I compare information.
How to walk a straight line and not err,
Not to splash into a mess.

More cards in hand — more chances
To react to reality,
Why, sometimes even without finances
Naivety wins.

What I can, I do from my side,
Such is my hunting of happiness,
Much depends on my step,
Though the lazy say: “Fate decides.”
