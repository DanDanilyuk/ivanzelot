You don't believe in ascension?
And in reincarnation?
And in a life without aging
Or in levitation?

You don't believe? I have ascended.
And returned to earth.
I've levitated my fill
And reincarnated,
Grown younger, surely.

You think I've gone mad,
You'll say, some kind of sect?
I simply landed,
Just stepped off the plane.
