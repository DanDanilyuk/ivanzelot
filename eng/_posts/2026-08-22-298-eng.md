Before saying "Farewell,"
Learn to say "Welcome."
Do not scatter, but gather,
Not "I lost," boast, but "I have."

When you have,
Then they have you too.
Is it not better to find
There, where they easily throw away?

A potential enemy
Became a comrade,
Because to his reproaches
You did not say "Farewell."
