Forgive me and do not judge me harshly
For jealousy, a confident stride.
I stamp the chosen path with my step.
Do not doubt — I will not let you go.

You are no hostage, no captive.
If I imprison you,
I will agree to free you at the last,
But who will tear you from the heart?

I will rejoice at your decision,
I'll do everything for your happiness,
I'll turn words into action,
My patience will bear it all.
