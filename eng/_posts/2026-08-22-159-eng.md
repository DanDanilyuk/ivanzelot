You'll raise yourself,
You'll throw yourself too.
You'll meet what you need,
Give in — you'll stop.

Look out — you'll see,
Listen — you'll hear.
Love — you'll forgive,
Knock — you'll stay the night.

Stumble — you'll FALL,
Want — you'll ask,
Push off — you'll jump.
Choose what you want.
