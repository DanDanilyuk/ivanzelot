Led around by someone —
Deprived of a foundation,
A Ukrainian
Without his native tongue.

A door without a lock,
A house without a door,
A mouth without a tongue —
Easy prey
For treacherous people.

A yard without a gate,
Through the bushes — without boots,
Without a lantern at night,
Without a soul.
Because the words are alien.
