Circumstances force or attract,
Am I here by chance or on purpose?
Masses of examples around hint:
Nothing arises without self-interest.

I haul for myself — I gather for someone,
I'll lose it so someone finds it,
What was seized I return from soil to soil
Under these or other conditions.

Meaning in the small and meaning in the great,
In the happy and in the bitter.
Yielding or ambition,
How not to agree — a mission.
