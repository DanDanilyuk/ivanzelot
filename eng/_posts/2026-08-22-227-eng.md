Adam works the wrong way,
And rests the wrong way,
His taste is bad,
He steps where he shouldn't.

“Time to change something.
So it might get better,
Let him at least start chopping?
What's a man FOR?”

“I've had enough of that garden.
Uproot the TREES!”
He goes. No choice —
Eve commanded.
