Nothing is eternal —
It appeared and vanished.
Summer flourished — and winter.
You can't bring it back — a pity.

Days, months, and seasons
Settle as silt in the well of time.
But having waited out their term,
They circle in repetition,

For they are resurrected again.
They have no end,
Though they die…
…Do you hear God's call?
