They knew the method,
They made the trouble.
In time they forgot about it.
They would remember again,
But they locked memory tight.

Someone took it
And, no one knows where,
Hid it so well —
No one will find it…

BURY the evil in yourself,
So that not only you
But others too
Could not find it.
