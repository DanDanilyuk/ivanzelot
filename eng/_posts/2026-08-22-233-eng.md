The waters broke,
Everyone scattered,
Labor began,
Will I be LATE?

In my hands
Two lives. What am I saying — three!
The laboring woman's groin can't hold,
And I still have to carry

To the operating room.
The two of them and myself.
If only not too late…
It's about to burst…
