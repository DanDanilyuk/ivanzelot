To justify a truth, white or black,
They launch counterpropaganda,
As a result of a treacherous storm
They receive a destructive karma.

Tense situations
Start wars.
Subversive provocations
Turn demonstrations
Into bloody slaughters.

Will or fate?
The enemy or you?
Sparking against each other
The worlds of contradictions.
