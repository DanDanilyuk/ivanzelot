Lottery of fates. A rocket flies toward the Dnipro.
A tree fell on a house,
Someone was lucky, and someone was unLUCKy —
Didn't even make it to the start.

Run or wait motionless —
Your personal lot will not pass you by.
Over the ocean, at work or at home —
Something very pleasant or sad.

Minutes and hours take off in alarm,
Panic in Florida.
Milton* carries great ruins —
A reminder to us on the journey.
______
* A hurricane in 2024.
