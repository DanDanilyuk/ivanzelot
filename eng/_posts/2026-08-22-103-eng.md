Do not resist the evil one
When he takes your shirt.
But do not let anyone
Dishonor your daughter.

Do not listen to counsel
To set up your neighbor.
Fear betrayal like fire,
Lest you give help to evil.

Be ready for sacrifice,
Give what is yours —
Prepare yourself to die,
And save the innocent.
