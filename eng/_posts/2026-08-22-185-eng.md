It will give fullness.
Will offer, refuse,
Agree, forbid.
Will set
A goal,
Whitewash, blacken.

The monotony of weekdays it will lift
And lower,
Inspire,
Lead
To a feat,
Drive laziness out,
Force into activity,
Turn a little boat into a ship.

It will take the newspaper and the smartphone.
Tear you from the television...
If not for the tree of temptation,
Who would we be, Eve?
