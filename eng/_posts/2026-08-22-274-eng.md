A surname has a reason
But not everyone knows it.
Yet on every person
The family brand has its effect.

Registered as Malomuzh
He feels himself small,
But why then
Does he pride himself as a king?

The power of the word
In cursing and in praise —
The ancestral foundation
Lives in you as well.
