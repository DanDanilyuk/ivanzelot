The viper flees
When God has commanded,
Or it bites.
Silently, without words.

Conscience is appointed
To explain to evil
About the immediate readiness
To cease sinning.

Listen to it,
Do not wait for the viper.
You will avoid the serpent,
The torments will be forgotten.
