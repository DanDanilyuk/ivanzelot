A bitten cat, or an aged one,
One wants to give back to mother nature,
So I will not be surprised when I too
Am not spared by fate —
Crippled and injured,
Thrown out when the occasion comes.

I will not complain of cruel loneliness
And of ungrateful nearest ones.
I will assess the situation from the other side,
To rise higher above the problem...

…The planet is trying
To see the sun
With human eyes and understand:
Its star has rejected it — what is this?
…What was born in a body returns back —
To explain it to the Earth.
