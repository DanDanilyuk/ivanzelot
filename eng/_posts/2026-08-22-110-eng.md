From edge to edge
A packed hall,
And I am singing,
I wait for no praise.

Oh, in the meeeeadow
The REEEED kalyyyyna…
I forgot about friends,
The wife slipped from mind.

All motion froze,
A DEAD silence came —
The spirit sings,
The soul has gone numb.
