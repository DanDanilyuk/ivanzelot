Ivan and Marusia have
A little daughter and a son.
Hence the nicknames – Marusyn
And Ivanova Yasmin.

In times of need for surnames
Nicknames were used,
So they would be easily recognized.
As they said, so they wrote it down.

Ivanova for the sister,
As Marusyn – the brother.
So that each one's family
Would be considered Russians.
