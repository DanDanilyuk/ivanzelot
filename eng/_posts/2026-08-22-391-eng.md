At the interrogation he whimpered.
He thought he had survived,
Kept his soul,
But his conscience - he could not.

So what did I win,
Having refused freedom?
I gave up my brothers-in-arms,
Hastening the defeat.

"So die twice —
For them and for yourSELF..." —
As a verdict in the face
The sky pronounces.
