God created a machine—
An independent human
And gave her the will
To remain herself.

Having renounced the Creator,
She named herself him,
She hurries to the end
Unsubmissive, insolent.

For the unfortunate one will meet
A ditch around the turn —
The traitress is waylaid by
Her artificial intelligence.
