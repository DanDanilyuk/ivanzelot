We left peace, toward peace we go
Through the tension between the poles,
To learn to avoid problems
According to the Creator's program.

A situation is a challenge. It freezes — it burns.
At once we need without-peka.
It is hard to hide, the threat grows,
From everywhere not-without-peka menaces.

One cannot do without not-without-peka,
It stimulates life,
Not WITHout not-without-peka they once fought,
Not without not-bes not-without-peka stands watch.

(A gag on the nonexistent word Peka)
