I invite you as a friend,
Though jealousy tears us both apart,
Between us extreme tension,
Each suffers deeply.

We emphasize artificial indifference,
We attract  with charisma,
We diligently flatter outsiders
With iron endurance.

With the intent to snap the nerve
Of the most important in the world
I am the first ready now
To fly headlong to you.
(Truth)
