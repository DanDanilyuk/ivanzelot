To savor-drink —
Value the processes,
Don't rush to the finish,
Don't haul – CARRY.

Choose a goal
Unattainable.
Assemble bit by bit  Deliberately.

And leave it to descendants,
So they would continue.
So they too would live.
So they would multiply,
So as not to END.
