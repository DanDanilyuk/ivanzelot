It seems that to poetry
The samurai still belongs.
Claims against oneself
Compose already in pair with him.

In the shortest form,
In a few lines,
In life's storm
In the most sparing words.

Better to forget about harakiri,
Whatever the tribunal might judge.
Is it not better to hear from fate
Of a victorious finale?
