You ride and do not know
Where it will fly in.
— You are playing with death, —
It drills in the mind.

A safari on people
Satan arranged,
In a frenzy
Called Ukrainians Satan.

At first glance
He divided his kingdom.
It will not
Stand at all.
But what are they doing here
The Ukrainians' ordeals?
From the curses of minions
Separate them.
