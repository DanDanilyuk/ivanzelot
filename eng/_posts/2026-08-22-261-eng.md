The echo of youth
So distant,
With sensitivity off the scale,
Not erased to the end.

From the very core,
From the center of the innards
It begs toward the surface.
Barely touches it
And back into the core
Is swept swiftly.

Into the very first cell
Of one's own being,
Into the first cause...
For all will perish,
And she lives.
