She worries whether I breathe with difficulty —
Cares, watches over my sleep.
Beside her, with a quiet anxiety,
I am ready to overcome a million
Obstacles.

Let her reproach that I ignore her,
That I love too little, forgot about her —
"Someone  needs me" — I'll feel once more,
I'll note the important thing at dawn —
And happily fell asleep again.

No small strength,
Filled with thoughts.
And though they aren't visible, be calm:
You haven't enough of me, you are lonely and tired,
But know: only you — the purpose and the essence.
