Tell yourself: it sucks,
And crap will crawl in from every side.
Proclaim: all is wonderful —
Then wait for happy changes.

The pleasure center is in safety
From words of encouragement
That guard its fortress.
They let no enemies near.

The irritant from outside will lose its force.
If the gates are under lock.
Weakened, the wings will flap
And the dragon will fly away.
