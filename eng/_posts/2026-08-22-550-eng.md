The lion of the empire grew hungry,
Went out hunting.
They grew alarmed in the prairie,
About survival.

They hoped the crocodile
Would rescue the antelope,
Or a herd of gorillas
Would solve the problem.

Justice froze,
Ran to the shelters,
The crocodile and the lion
Share the prey.
