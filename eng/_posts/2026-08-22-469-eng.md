Stuffed with clay or cotton
Even with a zealous attempt
Imagination will not explode,
You cannot split with the blunt of a log,
Nothing arises without a cause,
After hard fatigue it is pleasant to rest.

A reaction to a stimulus,
Coordinated behavior —
As the participant,
So the evaluation.

Sometimes to reach something great,
Seemingly even devoid of sense —
It is useful to know:
If it is the right,
Even a quiet
Word is enough to say.
