Friends —
The filling of a lack.
In a smoothie of downfalls —
The pledge of good luck.

What's missing — they give,
They lend a shoulder.
They carry the weak,
Blow on the wounds when they burn.

The egoist longs to TAKE,
Everyone serves him.
And are you ready to give?
Or do they only befriend YOU?
