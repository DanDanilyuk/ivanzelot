Here it is, approaching,
And I had not even guessed what to call it,
On what occasion
And what to expect.

Reached it? Hardly.
It knocked on the door itself.
I don't believe in projects –
For some it barely scratched quietly,
For some it stomped its feet.

Open — and you will see.
Let it in — it will ENTER.
Unobtrusively
Show hospitality —
It will lay out its gifts.
A calling! — so we shall say.
