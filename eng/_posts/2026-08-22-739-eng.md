Again relationships — a bottomless theme.
Grievances and insults in a sauce of justice,
Defending oneself, a zealous need
Not to miss a chance to boast.

Boast of what?
To put the neighbor down?
Or to sort through the labyrinths of causes
Of the wondrous things lived through?

Yes, I look for advantage,
Exits for myself and for those I speak with.
From disappointments and emptiness
Memory suggests the ways of flight.
