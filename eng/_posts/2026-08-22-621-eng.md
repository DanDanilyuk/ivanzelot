— I love you. ❤️
— And I you — not. ❌
So there is no reciprocity. 🏃‍♀️‍➡️
— I'd wish that you gave yourself to me, 🎬
Renouncing independence. 🕸️

— And I am looking for another 👀
With absolutely the same aim: 🥅
To have him as the obedient one, 🧎
And not become his slave. 🗽

— So maybe we can somehow agree? ❓
I'll become yours, and you mine, 🤝
And I, and you, will consent to the sacrifice,🔥
Calling ourselves a family of lovers? 👩‍❤️‍💋‍👨
