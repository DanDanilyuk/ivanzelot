The hungry is no brother to the full,
The prayer of the full
Is insincere.
The sated have more holidays,
For he is a grabber and a hustler.

When someone has, he is a bourgeois —
The Bolsheviks taught it thus.
Judge him, spit on him,
And hate with all your strength.

The American and the Jew
Are responsible for everything
(So the beggars believe).
They saved up money —
Now let them spend.
