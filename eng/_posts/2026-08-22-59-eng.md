Why do we need other
Civilizations,
If what we need is more
Militarization?

For star wars
The time has not yet come,
For billions together
So the atom may sweep them away.

But we study hard —
Bring on the cataclysms.
Now that will be a feast —
Some to hell, some to heaven.
