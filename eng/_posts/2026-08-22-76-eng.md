You wasted time —
You gave your life.
They paid you.
You did it in good conscience,
Cleaned everything up —
Spent your strength.

You live?
By what to measure?
Money — the standard.
Who are you?
To check,
The finance background will say.

Credit histories.
Such is the earthly template.
But accountable to heaven
Are the overcomings
Of obstacles in faith.
