What does rhyme do
In search of the truth?
In search of the truth
She is the reason.

She will climb upward,
She will dive to the bottom,
She will go beyond horizons,
She will tear the thorn,
And she will lay the path.

She will sweep away false stanzas,
Keep only one.
She will answer
For what I find.
