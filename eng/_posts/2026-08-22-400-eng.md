A person learns,
While alive.
Until it leaves them,
Their soul.

Spirit — the teacher,
The body — a vessel,
Bearer of the soul.
By souls we judge the body.

The soul will leave the body,
Will COME home,
Will meet the SPIRIT-Father,
And pass the exam.
