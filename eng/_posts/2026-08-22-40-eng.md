When you warm your chest
With blankets on the sheet,
A cold —
Is also a blessing in the Lord,

And also on the weekend
On New Year's night
There comes in a dream
No ordinary thing.

You take the phone
And write in VERSES —
A marathon of thoughts
You'll even leave to others.
