Language divides a country.
Does Kazakh really divide Kazakhstan?
Ukrainian — Ukraine,
Azerbaijani — Azerbaijan?
Moldovan — Moldova,
Latvian — Latvia?

Russian "unites" peoples,
If they don't want it — it will force them,
From it only "blessing, no harm at all,"
Everything foreign, it will Russify.

With genocides and provocations,
With repressions and deportations,
With Holodomors and wars
It will make us free.
It will denazify,
Appropriate and finish destroying.
