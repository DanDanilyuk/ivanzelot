My universe is in my projection,
There is no need to copy it
In order to recognize
Dementias in it.

My constellations draw daisies,
Trains and mountains.
Your imagination otherwise
Speaks of the sky.

Don't worry, friend,
Don't DIG in the worse —
You and I are not ill.
From your center differently
The stars are placed.
