The Lord accepts no burnt offerings.
Has He become a vegetarian,
Or have mortals shed their delusions
And the ritual of sacrifice fallen away?

They have become gods to themselves,
They bring praise to themselves,
Satan rubs his hands,
For Adam the frontier is already near.

If there is no Creator, sacrifices are not needed,
Steal, rape and lie.
You will have to die forever,
Write the sacrifice off to yourself.
