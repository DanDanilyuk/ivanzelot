What I didn't expect has happened,
Nobody could even invent it.
I counted the chickens in autumn,
The dearest thing I lost, I managed to caress.

The reward for dreams will not pass by,
Romance is a serious business:
Wherever you weren't going, it will lead you there,
What you never even dreamed of — you will TAKE.

They sense the end, they drop their hands,
The skyline becomes a wall —
For the despairing — a time of parting.
That's only the beginning for you and me.
