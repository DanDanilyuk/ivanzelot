Sodomy
Destroys society.
Same-sexness wants
To raise itself higher.

It degenerates humanity,
Advertises itself,
Spreads debauchery,
Ignores morality.

You are gentle toward it,
And it grows insolent.
It looks like a shoot,
Dreams of becoming a forest.
