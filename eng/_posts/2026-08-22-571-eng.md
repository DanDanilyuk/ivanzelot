I lived not that way and not toward there —
Again conscience judges the sinner.
There is strength, there is no goal —
The logistics broke.

I insured myself, peace of soul
I accepted in trust in God,
He dictated verses to me
And pointed the way.

Happy and sad
I return home.
Not satisfied with myself?
My fault:
I doubted in Him.
