The best warming station is at home.
And what if at home there is no warmth?
And you are needed by no one,
In autumn and spring, and in summer it is winter.

He carried firewood into the house, but it will not burn.
They will say: "That's the kind of master he is.
And let him not trouble our heads —
A wretched man, and we feel sorry for him."

But he will find a lonely one across the ocean,
At the edge of the earth, it is hardly believable.
Entirely by chance, by an absent plan —
And both will be warmed.
