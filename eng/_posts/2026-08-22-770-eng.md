One beats, seven hold,
And suddenly the victim rises in spirit,
The attackers flee —
The humiliated will take comfort and learn
That there is truth in the world.

They will support the weak
For will and for might,
Everyone loves victory
And a change of places.

Yesterday's hooligans
Are humane like no one.
They are us, you and I.
Good is cloaked evil.
