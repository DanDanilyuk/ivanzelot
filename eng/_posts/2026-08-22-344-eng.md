A question mark instead of a period,
Or a COMMA skipped,
Like a river with no crossing
On the way home.

As if you walked a straight line,
A tailwind blew —
And now, stand on the SHORE
Because of a dash, or colons among the letters.

Through a tiny mistake
They understood it wrong,
A punctuation mark
Made a halt.
