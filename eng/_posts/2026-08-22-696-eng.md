My word is — Glory!
Hallelujah to the Creator!
The work is finished —
I go to Him.

To the Lord God,
The Creator Almighty.
To the Holy Living One,
Judge and Savior...

The worthlessness will pass
Of mutilated fates,
Eternal bliss
The Password will unlock.
