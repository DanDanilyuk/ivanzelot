Who, if not Gog, heir of Cain, will avenge Cain too?
Abel will live to see the truth.
The boomerang of HATRED will fly back,
Though Cain might even have looked like a saint.

Did you indulge evil?
Did you love evil?
Did you pass the cup
To the bloodsucker?
Well, are you not Cain? Or have you not earned
The status of a killer?

Justice will RETURN
Home
From captivity,
But it will NOT do
Without Armageddon.
