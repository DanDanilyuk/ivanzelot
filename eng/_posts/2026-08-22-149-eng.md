Wolf and goat —
Aggressor and victim,
A hopeless tear,
The sated and the dead.

Goat and cabbage —
Vegetarianism.
Humanity's safety
Without wars and boorishness.

Cabbage and wolf —
Mutual indifference.
The rapist fell silent.
Doesn't roar and doesn't choke.
