To torment a cat —
Is to show love.
They pester me —
So they love me
(In order to add
Or, using me,
They will destroy me).

The two ends of opposition
Are in love as well —
The pleasure of courtship
Or the shedding
Of precious blood.

They love, sacrificing
And DEMANDING
A mutual sacrifice.
Gaining love,
Let us be ready to die for it as well.
