Homework done for the children
Doesn't solve problems,
But deepens them.
Hidden among the lazy
They don't hasten the good,
But call down misfortune.

Beloved son
(And thoughts of Ukraine),
Seek strength in yourself,
So they don't bite you,
Try yourself
To fight back the dogs traitorously
Set upon you.

In help don't blame
Someone else's delay.
Credit the victories
To your repentance from your own laziness.
