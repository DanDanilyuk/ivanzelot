Fertilization and death
Arouse in different ways.
In the through-and-through stitching
Birth and vanishing away.

It appeared —
To seize.
It yielded,
To set free.

The greater is formed by the lesser,
The cell becomes a body.
The last — again the first…
…After the fuller whole
The soul flew.
