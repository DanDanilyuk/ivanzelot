Many people wished a happy birthday.
More precisely, Viber.
Some in person, some from their families.
Because in the app a timer goes off.

Thank you, Viber, and thank you, friends,
Judges of my aging,
For remembering.
The count of years henceforth
For joy or for sorrow
Let them keep.

For it is pleasant for each
To feel
In a world of loneliness and in an anxious world
That there are many of us.
