"Russkiy mir" in all its "beauty":
"We need to see
How all of you are crying —
Let everyone suffer."

"That's their problem," they say,
"God loves us, for we are holy.
For they will bind the guilty,
We do the binding, so we're cool."

Blind "saints," blind murderers,
For the grief is in others, not in them.
The indifferent is ALSO a bloodsucker,
In untruth he ALSO has gone astray,
Also numbered among the wicked.
