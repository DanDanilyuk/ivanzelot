— How much is two plus three?
— It depends on the country.
Four where you steal whether you want to or not,
Six — in the one that does not give up its own,
Five — in a dictatorship of discipline.

And while algebra counts
In conditions of peace or war,
Whoever took power, he rules,
Though it devours and rages,
The horde with the zeal of locusts,
Offers a zero in the sum,
For it hates fullness.

Two and two —
Four.
By the look of it — simple arithmetic.
Two boards, a nail, — no hammer left…
Nobody swiped it —
Such is the mathematics of truth.
