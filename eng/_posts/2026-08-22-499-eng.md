If the Throne is a logical reality,
And not a religious invention,
They will judge the work as noble,
Not as a degenerate's cunning.

Not for a stranger's eye,
Not for earthly glory
Is high morality,
For them to lie with it.

The absence of truth in the world —
A quiver for the arrow
That will hit its mark
As the hunter aims it.
