Everyone takes on a mission
To serve God and the people.
A good one or a vicious one,
Hoping for a reward.

He loves or he HATES —
He considers himself righteous.
Here an arrow flies from him —
He looses it with love.

Good and evil have
Polarity.
Their charges change.
Loved his fill, and malice again —
And the stars are smiling.
