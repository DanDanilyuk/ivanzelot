The series is more interesting than one's own life,
Evening screens finally flare up,
The events of the day —
Useless trash,
Other people's fantasies — the source of mood.

Don't be ashamed, you are not a parasite,
And let conscience not judge you for the parallel:
The world is composed of overlays of realities,
And people lived this way before you.

They read books, wrote works,
Told stories, gossiped,
Don't worry, you are not sick either,
They simply did not have the internet then.
