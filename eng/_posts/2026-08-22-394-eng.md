Knowledge enriches, if you believe it.
To doubt it — it brings harm.
If it is convincing, it will force you to act —
Wait for the reward.

What does true mean, and what false?
Conscience decides.
Not dollars, not hryvnias,
But service to the truth —
All must die
And would that they reach salvation.

I am not trying to prove
To opponents, nor to myself,
I strive not to touch what is another's,
But to recognize my own.
To find my own paths.
