From a Chinese woman and a German
Born in France,
Who ended up in America,
What will he call himself?
To which nation will he belong?
How will he keep himself?

As an Indonesian
Or a tumba-yumba,
A Nigerian,
Or maybe Judas.

Yours
Or ours,
The humiliated most-just?
I have no doubt he'll choose the best —
The most promising.
