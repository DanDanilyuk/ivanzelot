"They did not love us,
They attacked us," —
In a fumes hangover
Failed explanations.

Did you not attack first,
To provoke the fury?
The propaganda fairy tale
Not everyone will take from you.

Truth has not died in the world,
And you will not kill it,
You monster of the adversary,
And in hell you will burn.
