Heated discussions
Are meant to develop,
Those bitten in arguments
Have grown used to biting.

Cutting off, they speak.
They talk in order to stop.
They bind the listeners
With the intent to lecture.

If only not to rush to the word
With sound and with letter —
The best conversation
Is conscience with thought.
