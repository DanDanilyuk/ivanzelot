Key-words
To the heart, to money, to flights
Lead out of various plights.
With passwords — one's own,
Without them — strangers.

Knowledge is words.
The initiated in them pass through.
No knowledge,
Then no success.
Whoever ignores them harms himself.

Hard to carry in the head —
A computer, a phone, paper
Will help you remember.
Passwords and key-words
Must be kept.
