Marked cards and an ace up the sleeve
Promise a win,
The assistants are his as well,
To develop the large stakes.

Fraud and deceit rule the world.
The main thing is to bribe the enemies,
And that he himself is bribed
He himself should
Forget
And tell
No one.

Ask the supreme one about the peak of the pyramid
On which he has perched so high,
Whether his empire
Will not ride down to hell
When the Most High's eye blinks?
