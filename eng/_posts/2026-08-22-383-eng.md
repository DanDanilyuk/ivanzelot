People crossed them out,
God received them.
They hewed a cross for the grave
To stand in memory.

They scratched in the name,
Born — died.
They had a bite and a drink,
And now what?

The dead have parted, drawn a border
By a bottomless crater,
They have hidden until their times
Behind a screen of secrets.
