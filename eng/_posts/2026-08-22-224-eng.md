Awakened by an idea,
Conceived in thought,
Expressed in sound,
Painted in a letter,
Sealed in an envelope.

Consent or coercion,
Construction or ruin,
Putting on or shaking out —
Materialization.

Denial or approval,
When they say: Done.
Fullness and glorification —
the WORD!!!
