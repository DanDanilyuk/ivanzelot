With leftover ego from the egg-egg
It befits a chick
To lecture the hens —
For it also to demonstrate
The potential of life.

Let it try, gain confidence
To spin the universe,
And the experienced wise would do well to avoid
The harmful jealousy
Of stopping children,
So as to resurrect in them
As a renewed  phoenix.

The mission is unfinished, admit it,
The peak of hopes has still not been reached.
Give the young one a push, do not hold back —
Pass the baton and do not begrudge it.
