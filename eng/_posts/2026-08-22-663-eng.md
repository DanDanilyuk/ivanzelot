Why Christ, if there is no resurrection?
Churches, synagogues, and mosques are needless too.
Do generations really live only to die —
For the triumph of "almighty" death?

At the finish — a zero — they did not believe God,
The Creator's plans and eternity,
Forgot they had the power to breathe,
Do not notice they are pregnant with themselves.

They have gathered to deny Life,
They give everything to ruin —
They will surely have to strike out
Even the fact that they live.
