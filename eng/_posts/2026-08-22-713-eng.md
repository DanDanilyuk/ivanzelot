Again the alarm vibrates:
"Enough sleeping, get up, rise
To the usual routine tasks —
With enthusiasm or complain.

Decide yourself, joy or sadness
Will lead you by the hand through the day."
I choose the better profit —
A thought inspired by the positive:

"I'll LOVE what I do, then I'll do what is loved."
From such a slogan and substance
Everyone will be well,
Not only me, the egoist.
