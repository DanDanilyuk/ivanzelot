(Beggar):
"They stole from me, I stole from myself —
It is called corruption.
I came to you,
And you lock yourself in."

(Rich man:)
"I am the master, I did not squander,
That is why the storehouses are full.
You drool over what is another's,
To go begging outside.

Where is the guarantee that if I give
You will not carve it all apart?
We will believe your words,
And then you will get it."
