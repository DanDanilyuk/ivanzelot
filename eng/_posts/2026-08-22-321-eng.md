The alarm is about to ring,
The doors into the day will open,
I wait for STRONG motives
For the birth of NEW songs.

Without the morning spark of meaning
Some activity will still get written down,
But the heavenly agency can see —
The passive will remain in place.

To shun dry inertia
Is what the everyday threshold asks.
To reach progression —
Where the body is, there spirit and soul.
