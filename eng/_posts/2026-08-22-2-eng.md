Archangel Michael
NOW in Ukraine,
9 months already.
In the east and in the south
he drives Putin's hordes.

I will not say that he is Zaluzhny,
but he is Zaluzhny's commander.
He makes the Ukrainian brave,
and the Horde's plan a failure.

Zhora-Heorhii
is blazing,
Michael waves his sword.
Palageya is BURNING.
They fight with heavenly fire.
