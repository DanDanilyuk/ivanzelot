Word-formation, mosaic, sacred sounds,
World-structure, a strict law —
The speaker takes the cosmos in his hands,
Tears it and forms his own canon.

The likeness of the Creator, like a spermatozoon,
Strives toward Truth from bottomless depths,
Wins immortality in a duel with zero,
Bounds up to unreachable heights.

The ovum waits powerfully,
Watches the vault of ladders and towers,
The Creator helps the thought of man,
Word with Word, for He too is the Word.
