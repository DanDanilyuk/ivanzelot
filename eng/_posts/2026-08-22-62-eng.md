Returned from captivity
At the start of the war —
You go home,
Where wife and sons wait.

When they released you
As the war flared up,
Rejoice they did not kill you.
But the meeting was sad.

If it dragged on,
You returned — and into the ranks.
Everyone turned away —
Traitors into battle.
