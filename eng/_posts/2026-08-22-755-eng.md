I have said everything, let time edit,
Its gap I filled as I could.
Another thinker hunts for meaning
While the threshold to the exit is still far.

He will research it himself, will not believe me,
To be convinced is everyone's duty,
Given the chance let him change the idea for the better,
Cleanse it of the slag of hostile deceit.

We search together in the earthly purgatory,
Not competitors, but co-workers,
There, beyond the threshold, having shed the rags —
Where, we will understand, and from where we are.
