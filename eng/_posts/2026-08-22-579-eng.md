Paradise exists after all.
How long is hell to torment us?
Peace comes someday.
And is it truly so far?

A kind word to a neighbor,
A memory of a happy dream
Strip away the oppressive extra,
Drive the darkness clean away.

I carry hell and paradise in me
In dreams and in waking.
Weakness in health, strength in sickness —
I myself choose what I live in.
