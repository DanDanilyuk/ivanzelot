On the canvas
It comes out better.
For life in its routine
Is harder and heavier.

The artist has pondered,
Filtered it,
The essential, compressed,
Laid it down in paint.

He sifted out the chaff,
Took away the wrinkles,
Where needed, intensified,
Where needed — hid.
