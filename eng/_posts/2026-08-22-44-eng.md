Oh, how many geniuses
Lie in the graveyards.
Waiting for prizes
In the heavens.

Trampled by their contemporaries,
Mocked and tortured to death,
Hanged and drowned
And honored by the Creator.

They died in want,
Even the names were gone,
"Wiped out" and forgotten…
We follow after THEM.
