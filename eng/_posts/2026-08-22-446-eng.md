Where you took,
There you will have to return.
Where you invested,
From there you will also take.
Not always at once, — sometimes one waits long,
But you will not bypass justice.

You sowed good –
Bread will come floating in,
Misfortune on your head — for former evil.
Contempt for father and mother
Will turn descendants into a nest of snakes.

Boomerang and karma —
Words foreign to us,
But cause and effect are easy to understand.
Tomorrow the present poor
Will grow rich.
After winter, come flying, stork.
