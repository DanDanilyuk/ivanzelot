Cinema is also reality,
And books, in their way, carry destinies.
Every passion or specialty
Offers a separate actuality and essence.

Recollection is yesterday,
Imagination paints tomorrow.
And the present?
How do I see it?
Does the soul have it, or HAD it?
Will I not lose what is received today?

Fantasy and memory, past and future,
The other's and one's own — galaxies many.
Even if they are torn to pieces —
Apart and together — LIFE!!!
