He built a fine house,
She treated the sick,
Gave a million to the ZSU,
Fed the homeless.

Pilot, doctor, pianist,
Locksmith, nanny, journalist,
She had a gift for painting,
She was a true optimist.

Death took them… Let it take,
Let the body rest…
But their work lives on —
Drawing us nearer to paradise.
