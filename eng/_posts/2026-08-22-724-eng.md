When on the road the steering wheel jams
And a sharp afterburner is called for,
An ordinary brake comes to the rescue,
To preserve the crew.

It is not speed that causes the accident,
But a sudden stop.
What shatters the relations between a couple
Is not a powerful blow, but the fragile
Perception
Behavior:

Indignation, jealousy, offense,
The painful desire to take revenge,
They could not at the start, at once
Pause the emotions —
The result is weeping,
Not a yielding ball,
But a stubborn smashed vase,
You cannot glue it back together.
