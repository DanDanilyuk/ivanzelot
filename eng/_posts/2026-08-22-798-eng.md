Drop me and let me go,
It is not you in my thoughts now,
Why waste a life?
You let go, and I will fall in love again.

Darling, I have found love,
I long for her again and again.
For warm lips. Yours are like ice.
Beloved, we ought to part.

DROP me and realize:
Love harms us bOOOOth,
For us to live together is the family's pain,
You let go, I will love her.
(Don't hold on. Let go!)
