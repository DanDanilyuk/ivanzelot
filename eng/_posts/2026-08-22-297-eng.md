Remain the owner of your sword,
Let it grow into your palm.
Let it become native to your shoulder,
To keep you from slavery and captivity.

Quotes of geniuses — others' shields.
It is good to know them
And to remember.
But forge your own slogans,
Let others remain alive,
So that you too need not die.

What is borrowed will cripple, will cut.
Come out of battle the victor,
Cultivate your own, reliable and fresh.
Hold the handle and be yourself.
