Muse of muses, Music, queen of sounds —
Cacophony of nature, of animals and people.
The solemnity of meetings, the ache of parting.
The grandeur of thunder and the creak of a door.

It aches in the chest,
Tears with adrenaline,
Hums through weekdays,
Roars on Sinai.

The smash of the Universe,
The language of neutrons,
And without Adam's babble —
the Demiurge's Word.
