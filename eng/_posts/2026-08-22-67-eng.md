The bones already ache,
Tired of lying,
Rested enough already,
Time to get up.

Zealously to the work,
To overturn mountains,
Until you fall,
Until grief befalls you.

But to be ill is also a blessing.
Thought works,
In fog or thirstily
It ponders eternity.
