Gaze at the feet, and thoughts up above.
A preoccupied world,
And in it a bubble.
Such are my thoughts of Diana,
Everyone has bags, and she has a little purse.

Inquisitiveness and curiosity,
A full bosom of dreams,
And with them even
The surname Riy as well.

A swarm of wishes and strivings,
Of hopes and trusts.
So I wish you achievements,
Diana Riy.
