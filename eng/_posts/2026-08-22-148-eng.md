And one in the field is a warrior,
If Samson's spirit is in him.
He will find the needed weapon
For attack
And for defense.

But the greatest power is held
By the tongue's two-edged sword –
The word shatters walls,
When you speak the truth.

Before it the liar and the thief
Drop their hands.
5 persons or 6 peoples
Cast aside arrows and bows.
