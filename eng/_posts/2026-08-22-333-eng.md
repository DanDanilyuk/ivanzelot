Shocked, captivated, fused to him with attention.
I share with others — a sensation.
I tear myself from my affairs, I pull toward him.
But what a talent — such ovation.

And suddenly a letdown — the idol declares:
A subscription for him, likes and comments,
And he blesses the SVO,
The victims' arguments do not trouble him.

It is disgusting to look at a narcissist.
Love and adoration shatter to bits.
Now I am sorry —
Let the scoundrels be in love with him.

(Disappointment in celebrities)
