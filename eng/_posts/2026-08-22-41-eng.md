Your video greeting card
I am already seeing a third time.
I hoped that, a little drunk,
You'd put in something of your own.

A scrap of heart and soul
Is what the owner's word gives.
Whether prose or verse —
If only it is yours.

The artificial — dead,
A copy — a corpse.
A living sacrifice —
In communion, a marriage.
