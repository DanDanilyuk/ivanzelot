Give me, please,
A piece of your life —
Energy and time.
I'll share them too.

Aid and groceries,
A service and goods,
A horse for rent, a bay —
From me — a fee.

If not barter, then cash,
A promissory note — for a freight car.
The best dollar or kopeck
To trade lives is the standard.
