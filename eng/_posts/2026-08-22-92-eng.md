If it wakes you in the night,
Then it is truly important.
Selected thoughts will
Undermine
The impossible.

Chaotic worries of yesterday,
Cleansed by sleep of silt,
Will push you back to work,
Will resurrect yesterday's idea.

Yesterday will continue tomorrow
More focused and stronger.
And the cause — a perfected mantra,
Hewn by a livelier word.
