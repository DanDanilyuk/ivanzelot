I'll agree with a deviation to the right,
And how else is the planet to go?
Let us submit to authority, but not slyly.
In common sense — forward! To the goal.

So that, shifting the body's center,
We do not turn into the ditch,
Not stumble, not fall,
Not smash what we carried,
Feel again
That conscience survived,
Not lie, not strike
A compromise with evil.

No way without reforms, let us avoid stagnation,
To overcome new obstacles
You and I will think together as well,
To support the government, as a wise people.
