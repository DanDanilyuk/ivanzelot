Anger boils over, I'd tear the eyes out,
To scold is too little, I'd curse,
But it stubbornly will not apologize —
I'd tear it to pieces.

Eh, I'll ignore it.
Why ruin the nerves?
I pretend I neither notice nor hear,
Let them heal the breaks in the relationship.

Then I decide to meet in peace,
The potential enemy is embarrassed. We manage to listen and come to understanding —
Friendship grows even stronger.
