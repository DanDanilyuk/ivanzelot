Trust,
But verify —
There are plenty of speculators around.
Care about the quality of projects,
So that nothing would steal from them.

Devotion to the cause —
The guarantee of success,
Oversight and accountability bring victory.
You'll deprive competitors
Of a malicious smile,
Having DEFENDED freedom. And revenge is not the point.

Light for the maps, and it's on the field,
Night-vision glasses will help.
To stop and curb war and evil —
Such is the purpose of good and light.
