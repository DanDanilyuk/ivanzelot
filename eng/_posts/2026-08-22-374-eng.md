Truths are many,
The Truth is one.
What a feast this is —
To touch the bottom,

To climb Everest,
To rise to the orbits,
And to crawl, in the end,
To swim through, to fly through.

But again among the truths,
Having found the Truth,
Send it too among the truths —
The doubtful, the uncomprehended.
