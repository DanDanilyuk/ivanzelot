The opponent cut the talk short:
— You have horns, hooves and a tail.
They took away the chance to explain —
Anger is tearing me apart,

Though horns, hooves or a tail
I never noticed on myself,
The matter is not simple now —
They will no longer be friends with me.

They "saw through" me,
Exposed "satan,"
Spread the rumors,
Believed the liar.
