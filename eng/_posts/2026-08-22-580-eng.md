A festival of old friendship
And of new acquaintances —
Escape from civilization.
The public one will feel a loner,
The solitary will take ovations.

A plunge into the unusual,
The calloused changes its skin,
The magnificent becomes modest,
The spiritless receives faith.

Freshness of emotions —
Drunk on freedom.
Here it is, the blessed calm.
Welcome! You are born again.
