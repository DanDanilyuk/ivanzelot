Yes, there is a problem.
Does it really belong to us to avoid it?
Is it true that from one who bites and beats
The only way is to flee at once?

From a bear, a human, from the viper of fears?
Demons cackle, hell's messengers.
But having recalled the power of words,
Diplomat-rebels grow up.

They build dams, they fly into space,
They overcome separation from the neighbor,
They tame evil and no longer tremble,
To shake God's hand.
