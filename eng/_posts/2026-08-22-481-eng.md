Live a lifetime, learn a lifetime.
That is, take from those around you.
Knowledge will pile up someday
With the need to speak it out.

And even if no one needs it,
What was said to oneself
Will go out and come home
In more perfect phrases.

Take from others, honor teachers,
Give thanks for their wise words,
So that at last you too understand:
The best counselor for you is you.
