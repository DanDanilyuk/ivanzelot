As long as in the aggressor's tongue
We do not cease to speak,
The horde will persistently
Not stop “liberating” us.

It is wrong to stand out
In a land native to others,
In Germany as a Russian,
In Russia as a German.

Nor as a Muscovite in Ukraine
With Cossack roots —
In a forgotten tribe
As rotten seed.
