Covering the sick,
Soaked with blessings
And imperishable wishes
The healing robe
Gave the possibility
To go on living,
For someone recognized
The secret of prayer.

Full of hints,
Science teaches,
Having shed the mysteries —
See, it mixes the medicines.

The fleshly with the spiritual
Wars for the truth.
And a kerchief or a ribbon,
Or even a rag
To those full of faith
Grants health.
