Gog and Magog conduct a filtration,
Remaining of them
Are the most worthy:
The unreliable replenish emigration,
Fall under liquidation,
Considered as their own
Are the most cruel.

They will be able to "repeat,"
They will find adherents throughout the world,
They will gather ones like themselves
Scoundrels of various grades.

A few more steps to Armageddon —
As a world team of orcs
A herd of beast!s and animals
Satan in a single stream
Prepares in a mass
Upon Jerusalem.
