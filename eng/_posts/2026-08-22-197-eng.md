Everyone has their own experience
And each is wise in their own way.
Depending on enthusiasms and on events,
I judge someone, or I myself stand accused.

From what I understand I try to advise,
I take hints from those who know better —
Only together will we manage to get along,
Where to grow the bark, where to strip it.

I hope I am needed by you,
As you are needed by me.
While we live (as if),
While you and I are earthly.
