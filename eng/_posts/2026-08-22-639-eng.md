Stages of war, like classes in school,
Must be passed to receive a diploma —
In the epicenter, in the rear, abroad
To choose between evil and good.

To allow a dictator to take hold,
To agree to become a slave,
To surrender to propaganda for the assault,
To croak as an occupier, a dog.

To save yourself and your nearest family,
To settle, to recognize the possibilities,
To protect the country from the invasion
With healthy and wise activity.
