The world bustles, uncoils its tentacles,
Reaches toward heights and latitudes,
Does not give up, will not yield to the elements,
To win — a care of cares.

And the embryo in the embryo froze motionless,
In microscopes you can't even make it out,
Where that Beginning is, still unknown,
Eternity's rebuses play with us.

The boys fight, measuring strength,
For the girls, love is not enough,
Tentacle and embryo
Will surely win —
The masculine and feminine principle.
