Though it keeps on crawling,
The funeral procession,
To think about death —
Is not depression,

When you take death
As the door home,
When you wait from heaven
For the archangels' bell.

The earthly, the temporary
Goes quickly and slick.
So let us remember again:
The resurrection is near.
