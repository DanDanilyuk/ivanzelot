Trap.
Betrayed.
After long years of faithfulness.
She does not know yet — I have already lost her…
I did not expect such baseness of myself.

A fog dragged over my mind,
A chance beauty drew me in,
No words can cover the deceit,
And without my destined one I will not live.

The whole world is indifferent,
Only she alone
Takes care of me.
Oh, how the heart tears and grieves —
She will lose her soul,
My beloved wife,
When she is disappointed in me.
