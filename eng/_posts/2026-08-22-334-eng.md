The overlay of time maps
Reveals an error,
A cunning plan becomes obvious,
In comparison — who is worth what,
The difference of handwriting stands out,
How far deeds correspond to words.

But one condition
Is obligatory —
Open transparency,
At least partial,
And better still, with the light of clarity from below.

And the answer is ready:
Did they spread blessing or summon a crisis,
Treachery or conscience,
Pattern or chance?
