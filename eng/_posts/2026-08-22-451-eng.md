The imagined strikes sparks,
The imagined puts out fires.
The imagined smashes boundaries,
The imagined wants to sit down.

The imagined weeps,
The imagined smiles.
The imagined offends,
The imagined takes offense.

What to imagine,
I must choose:
What to caress,
And what to drive out of myself.
