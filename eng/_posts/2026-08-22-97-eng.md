You cannot keep up...
Where then is that time
To gather?
And do you even know?
What you spend it on?
Which route you chose,
What to attain?

Work… Work...
And what for?
In cares
Vanity,
Glory in foolishness,
In the hurries
Wastes on the wind.

Stop and think.
You will save your strength.
If you are wise,
Having driven out doubt,
You will come to the grave.
