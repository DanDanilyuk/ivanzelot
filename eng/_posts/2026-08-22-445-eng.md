The good and the evil
Across the face of the Earth,
Around the Sun and in the orbits of Galaxies
On different scales of different glory
Accomplish grand and tiny deeds,
Where the MegaUniverse is a worthless scrap.

Where a bacterium cannot imagine
The boundary of a stadium.
It hates, it loves,
It hurries home.

Where atoms and electrons
Have their own measures of time —
By their laws
I enter the class,
To understand the surroundings and the interior,
In harmony with them to serve the Plan.
