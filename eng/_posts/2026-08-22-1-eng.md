Nostalgia of memory,
the sovok holds.
Locked into the past
they lunged ahead.

The old ones lost their way,
the children will find it.
Hopes have shattered,
the grandchildren will gather them.

We sigh for what was lost —
laughter for the young.
We weep.
They are merry.
