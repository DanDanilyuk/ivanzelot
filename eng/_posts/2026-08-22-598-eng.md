(Sarcastic)
Do not divide into west and east.
Little Russia is a single country,
Speak only Russian.
The native tongue is not for Ukraine.

Do not divide into west and east,
TOGETHER surrender yourselves to the orcs,
You will suffer more troubles,
Wider streams of blood.

Do not divide into west and east,
The Kremlin liberates you
From language, freedom, and cities —
Luhansk and Donbas await you.
