The Prince of Peace was born —
Bayonets down.
The drone stopped,
Turned for home.

Even for a day, whoever is faithful
Will not kill, will not rape.
He'll remember that he is peaceful,
And joke once a year.

Christ was born
To die in glory…
… So the soldier too crossed himself
In the arms of death.
