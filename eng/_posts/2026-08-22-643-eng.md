Putin — "Moses, who to the promised land
Leads his great people,"
Having stood Truth on its head,
The laws, they say, are changed by the Lord.

Kill, steal and lie,
Covet another's goods,
Humiliate mother and aged father —
For greater holiness sin more.

Light at night, where a drone exploded.
A cloud by day, where ruins smoke —
Gog and Magog, Armageddon
Bring enormous changes.
