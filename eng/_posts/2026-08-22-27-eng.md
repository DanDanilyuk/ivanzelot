I was born to suffer,
To learn to give thanks.
So I will love
Instead of mourning.

Thank you for blessings,
Thank you for destitution.
For someone's respect,
For spiteful grimaces.

For beatings and medicines
As a sign of love
I must rejoice.
Thank you, God, thank you.
