If they accuse you again
Of harm committed by someone else,
"From a sick head onto a healthy one" —
That is how the people recognize it,

Nobility and love of neighbor,
Readiness for self-abasement —
Are not always incense for the Most High.
And abuses need a cleansing.

You endured till now — a reward awaits,
But now you will act wrongly
If you do not stop the sly one —
God will ask.
