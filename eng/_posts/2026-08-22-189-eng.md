Not having left yesterday's,
And with it the present,
You will not enter tomorrow.
And the flesh trembles.
Having stumbled somehow,
You will find yourself under the grass.
The morning
Will remain nocturnal,
For a long time  (or maybe for a moment)
You will shut yourself from the sun.

In the grave there seems to be decay.
Yet the fear of death
And the soul's agitation
Hint at the Resurrection.

The personal, intimately, not in public,
Sits invisible in the body,
Will someday disperse everywhere,
When it flies off to the stars.
