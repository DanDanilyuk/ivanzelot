«Украина — это Россия»,
Because it's written in Russian.
Russia — not Ukraine,
Perhaps we'll leave it at that.

Maybe enough of fraternizing?
From Stockholm syndrome
Isn't it time to return —
From slavery home?

Do slaves dream of becoming
Slave-owners
Over contemporary countrymen?

Apparently so,
For foreigners
Beat them,
And Ukrainians,
Having acquired the taste,
Filled to the brim,
Drink their own blood.
