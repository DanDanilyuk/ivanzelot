The essence matters,
Intuition will choose the style.
From within the words flow,
They pass by rust and rot.

Iamb, or trochee
Freestyle or canon —
Primitive for people.
Without obstacles.

The soul was bursting —
He didn't hold the doors.
She didn't worry —
Iamb or trochee.
