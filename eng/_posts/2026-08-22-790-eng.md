More than I can give,
Don't hesitate, take it, I allow,
I don't know
How else to prove
That I truly love you.

A sin, when there are bans,
A breach in the vow of fidelity, —
I open
The borders for a time,
You may take the most secret.

Fill yourself with happiness to the brim,
Break free without end,
Don't be afraid.
Come back again
And share —
I am waiting.
