Believe in the good, remembering the evil.
You, after all, while learning to solve hardships,
Came to school for a diploma.
They brought you to kindergarten, without even asking you,
Initiating you
Into the foundations of education.

Handing you to the world
With its problems,
At first for play, a fight with a shadow,
And at the exit with aggression, death, and laziness —
Radiance with the dark forces.

For the light to hide —
Means to go out,
To support evil by inaction.
So stand before the challenges of today,
That good may without doubt prevail.
