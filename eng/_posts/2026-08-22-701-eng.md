Without truth they walk in darkness,
They break and they beat,
Only by it all the earthbound
Will rise above the WORLD.

And you, of the worthless philosophers,
Neither Odysseus nor Heracles,
Shine in the dark tunnels,
The heroes are others, you are the oracle.

Their weapons — hands and feet,
Saber and horseshoe.
You have MORE power —
Receive the force of the WORD.
