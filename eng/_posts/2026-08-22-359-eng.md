Before the dawns the rains sprinkle —
They consecrate the earth.
Enrich it with heaven
After rest in the night.

The dust of yesterday,
That dealings stirred up,
They beat down, so a new trampling
May stamp its own patterns.

After the fantasies of dreams
Leap from the bed —
Let even from your footprints
A path emerge.
