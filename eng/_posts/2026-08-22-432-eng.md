Did Jesus appear to work miracles —
One levitation would suffice.
Pin Him to the cross —
Mortal or God — let Golgotha decide.

Once is not enough. Again and again
They kill the Lord.
They watch for the Messiah, that he would come
To suffer once more, as if on a schedule.

Recognition of the Sacrifice gives LIFE,
Doubt about eternity deadens.
Unbelief's great force
Hurries to destroy below.
Therefore the called have invisible wings —
So the soul would fly to heaven for salvation.
