The smart one knows,
The wise one uses.
It begins with knowledge,
And with knowledge it ends.

A spermatozoon knows
How to fertilize,
An asteroid knows
How to burn to ash.

The beginning knows
How to become the finish.
The living dies,
Because it knows
How to resurrect.
