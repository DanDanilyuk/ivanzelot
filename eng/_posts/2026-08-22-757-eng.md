Patience builds the will

One — a case, two — coincidence,
Three — a system, four — a law.
Where to stop, let conscience say,
Or to stretch out past the horizon.

Patience builds the will,
The breaking of relations — a crash,
I want to walk beside you,
Holding the path firmly.

In stumbles I will support you,
No one will smash, no one will fall —
Love is the cause for me.
Please understand me.
