Infectious refuse
Was added to the air
By the Master of life.
He sent a pandemic —

A violation of measure,
The destroying angel,
A test of faith
For every inhabitant.

Will they tell where the fictions are,
Human, or of the gods?
Will they run to a doctor,
Or to the Physician of physicians?
