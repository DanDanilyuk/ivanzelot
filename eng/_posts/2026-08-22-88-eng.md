She is his bone.
Love calls
To him only —
Her pull toward the man.

Her pull toward the man
Has the nature of glue,
That he should cling to her —
She needs protection.

That is why she is a woman,
To keep him at her side.
Her pull toward the man,
For she longs to rule over him.
