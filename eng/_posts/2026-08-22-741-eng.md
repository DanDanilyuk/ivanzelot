The unknown frightens.
The future lies in wait
Behind a screen.
Faith does not know,
But it promises,
Looks
Sincere.

There is something to recall of what was lost,
Reliable accounts in the banks,
And tomorrow, still unseen,
Waits beyond the nights in the daybreaks.

Shall we ignore the landmarks,
The callings, the purposes of living?
There is no getting by here without faith —
It is good to love faith,
Faith needs to be grown.
