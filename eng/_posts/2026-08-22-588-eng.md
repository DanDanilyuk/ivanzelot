Omnipresent egocentrism
Grants rightness to everyone,
Avoidance of crookedness
Gathers teams in the similar.

Alcoholics, sport,
Scientists, politicians
Unite in different spheres —
Masters of practice and of sophistry,
Specialists on paper.

Personal confidence
Enriches the social.
Weak limitation
Grows into a strong one.
