When we must, let us not shun the maidans,
To serve the people — is not to serve dictators.
The time comes to cast off the chains
Under the banner of freedom.

When a leader wants it,
But God does not command,
There is no choice
That can satisfy both…

Knowledge is flexible,
To err is unseemly,
Let us distinguish greedy human
From eternal blessed Divine.
