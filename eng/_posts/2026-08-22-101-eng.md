A premonition
Need not come true.
Sometimes words
Are allowed to be wrong.

You seek the truth —
Sift through the options
With intentions that are pure.
In the trash, diamonds.

You'll get mired in mistakes,
But you will find the truth
And never thank it enough
For the advice that saves.
