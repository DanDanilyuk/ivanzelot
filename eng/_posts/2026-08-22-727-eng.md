China waits. They say, it is wise.
Why should the Yanks hurry too,
In the Europeans' opinion,
To stick their nose into Europe?

All the more so, of their own
Problems they have a full sack,
And other people's fires
Are easier to put out
When beyond the borders
You do not have to go far.

Look, Europe is next to Ukraine,
So it is for her to help Ukraine,
And for America from across the sea
To supply Europe.
