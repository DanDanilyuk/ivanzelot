A jealous God toward traitors,
Cruel to the wicked.
He stops the attackers,
Strengthens the weak.

He accepted sacrifices
Until He sacrificed Himself,
Abolished burnt offerings,
Does not want the dead.

Neither dead love,
Nor dead goodness,
Nor cunning in the word
When the soul is dead.
