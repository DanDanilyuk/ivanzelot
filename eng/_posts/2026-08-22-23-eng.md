Language never taught us humanity,
So maybe the tongue will teach?
Let chaff feed us
From screens, speakers, and books.

Language will become unnecessary,
We'll start speaking with the tongue.
By the force of the ruskava word
Prague and London vazmyom!

We'll forget everything Ukrainian,
We'll renounce our fathers.
We'll swell the orc army.
To kill our brothers for the language.
