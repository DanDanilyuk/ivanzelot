At three thirty-three awakened,
By four it was put into verse.
By half past four I was moved —
Fixed by consciousness.

Conscience was whispering
In the night silence—
The Word was sifting
Nectar for the soul.

I edited till five,
I go on sleeping.
From fate, taken by the reins,
I wait for the results.
