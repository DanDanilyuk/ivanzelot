With the help of Hebrew
An explanation of the world.
Thanks to Latin
Order in medicine.
Esperanto appeared,
But it did not take root.

Every village has its own speech,
Every region — its dialect.
Each century alters the language,
And the present one offers its own project.

The recalling of Babylon —
A return home,
To the first source.
To mutual understanding,
To the Creator — creation...
Nobody is interested —
It is pointless to search.
There is no paradise left, and no tower.
