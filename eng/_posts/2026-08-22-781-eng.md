Humaneness loves, does not offend,
Breathes happiness, carries blessing,
Sets itself aside
And helps
Lift up the weakened other.

In conflicts, as a sanguine, it saves the truth,
Wins,
The one who took
And the one who gave,
All make peace, no one wages war,
In the courts it never comes to filing cases.

But when with pitchforks brother rises against brother,
Possessed in an evil frenzy,
It is time to call the heavenly ambulance,
And return God's morality to both,
To heal
The sapiens in the homo.
