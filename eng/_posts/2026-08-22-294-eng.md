Looked — does not mean saw,
Listened — did not always hear.
You say you did, but actually botched it,
You think you lived, — consider that you were.

To see — to understand,
To hear — to take interest,
To create — to want.
To live — to glorify

God and neighbor,
Solitude and the world,
Not to gather the superfluous,
But to bring forth fruit.
