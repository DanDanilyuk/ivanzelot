Inspiration took a day off.
Not because it was tired,
But I was sad —
So it grew sad too,

And I slept as if well,
The dream did not trouble me much,
Yet something answered in memory,
The word stumbled,
Not so brave anymore
From the insults of obstacles.

Not mine, but aimed at me.
I was not right for someone.
Inspired word,
Forgive me, I am angry at myself.
