Someday others too, without mountain passes, at once
Will go through the tunnel happy,
Saving shoes and time,
For the long miles will shorten.

And the highlanders will rejoice
On the age-old slope,
That their countrymen,
Not exhausted and dear,

Do not stumble and do not fall.
Though in plenty and in strength,
They do not even remember
The miners in the mile.
