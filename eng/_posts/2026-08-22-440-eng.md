Unattainable star…
Shepherd and king
On summit and throne
Are wrapped in unease —
Primitive desires turn immodest,
Steps leap beyond the horizon.

The present does not satisfy.
We call artificial intelligence to our aid,
Listening to the Primordial
Provides a safety net,
So that wonders may be brought to the status of the ordinary.

Despite all the failings
As a symbol of eternity
The three tries
To become an eight.
It inflates,
The kopeck dreams of growing into a hryvnia.
A finger stretches toward the finger of God,
To merge with the Creator one like the Creator.
