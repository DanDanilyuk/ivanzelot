Past, present and future
Have their components.
Events real or absent
Are transmitted in the word.

A constant process or a fragment,
A matter completed or continued,
Divided into shards or multiplied,
A millennium or a moment.

The probable, the impossible and the desired,
The imagined and the fact —
Conditionally laid out in tenses,
So as to taste the flavor of being.
