In prayer we ask —
We disrupt God's plans.
We bring thanksgiving —
However we wail,
We compel Him to nothing.

Thy will be done —
We say in the Our Father.
There is no our I —
Instead of doing, we lie down.

It is a sin to shirk cooperation,
Knowing the Lord's intentions,
Cooperation is necessary,
Reaching the goal together with Him,
