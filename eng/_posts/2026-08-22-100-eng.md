Is it better in fascist shit
Than in a democratic war?
Will the slave and the serf
Easily bypass death,

Remaining alive,
To smoke and smolder,
To the “elder brother”
To pay the tribute?

In their own hut
To make the bed for the lackey,
Besides unpaid labor
Also to wars
To go in his place?
