You gather treasures for yourself —
They will FIND OUT and come for them.
You meet someone else's needs —
The grateful will carry you in their arms.

Fate itself decides
Whether you fall back or hurry forward,
You keep when there is enough.
They ask — then you give.

But if the market is overflowing
And nobody needs a thing,
Then the time of rest has come.
You may even stay home awhile.
