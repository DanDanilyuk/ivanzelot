New technologies
Always frighten.
The unknown
Disturbs, terrifies.
Before labor
They wait for pain,
What is conceived gives birth —
It hurts a great deal.

What is too high
Threatens to fall,
The deep
Lures into the abyss.
The eye does not see —
It fears to give itself away.
The wide
Hurries into a journey.

To cover the distance,
To know the new,
To tear the fears,
To take what is hidden.
