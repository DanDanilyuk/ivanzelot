You will not lie long in bed,
The body needs motion.
A thought, by idea, by word,
The spirit suddenly wakes.

It would seem everything works,
Nothing flows anywhere.
NEW ideals are painted
By the soul, for it still wants more.

First it will feed the body,
And then it will TAKE UP the spirit.
The soul cannot go without work,
Its element is perpetual motion.
