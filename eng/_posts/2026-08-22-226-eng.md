Why Caligula? It simply appealed,
And Lucifer —sounds even better…
What we name ourselves, that we grow to like,
Having left fate to fend for itself.

Until it surfaces on its own,
Or you inquire
About the Child's Name.
With despair
It will suddenly pierce you,
And you will repent
With a belated conscience.

Pleasant to the ear
And a fine meaning,
But an evil spirit in it,
Karma invoked.
