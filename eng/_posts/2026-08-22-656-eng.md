On tiptoe on the big toe
A pirouette begs to happen —
Hopeless at an ordinary waltz
He strains toward classical ballet.

The spinning top laughs at challenges
With perfect balance.
Under cries of "Bravo!" —
To the end and with vigor.

I cannot,
But I want it badly.
They say I dare not,
Yet I will slip through.
