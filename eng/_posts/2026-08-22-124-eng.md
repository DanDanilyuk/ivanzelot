They advise not to hurry,
He who endures is saved.
Must we always endure?
As we sleep and as we walk?

Endure running
And endure sitting.
Endure holding on,
Letting go — endure.

Patience – in persecution
And in the bliss of the goal.
Endure in impatience
And in patience endure.
