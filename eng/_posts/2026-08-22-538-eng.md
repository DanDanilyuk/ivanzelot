An arrow is aimed point-first into the sky,
It hangs in the clouds like a kite.
It does not fall to earth, is not disappointed,
Guided by the inspiration
Of hopes
And dreams.

Where to get the fuel?
What is the idea?
Life has taken an interest —
It flies home,

To break through
From the human shell,
To reach the cosmic
Egg cell.
