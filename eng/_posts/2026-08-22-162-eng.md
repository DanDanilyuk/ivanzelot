First they went feral,
And then they became dogs.
They call black white,
They take everyone for enemies.

They maul their neighbors without mercy,
And wonder greatly:
Why aren't they loved
And no one is friends with them?

More than once they were believed,
Helped in times of trouble.
But the orcs wanted too badly
For their «velichiye»
To be recognized in them.
