Life and death in the stadium
And for the stands, where the spectators —
Two-sided confrontations
Concerning every soul.

Wherever you are — a contest is on,
Two boxers — good and evil.
In the ring: Hatred-Love.
Alone and in the mass — who takes whom.

Yet for a bright show it is fitting
To award medals to both,
For without problems a student will not learn
And will not receive a diploma.
