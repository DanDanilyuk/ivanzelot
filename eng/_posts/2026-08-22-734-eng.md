Dreams trouble, friends turn away,
And later depression torments.
In an enchanted circle find themselves
Rage and destructive aggression.

Everyone around is guilty,
The ego justifies itself.
And suddenly here — the Word,
Unobtrusively again and again,
From within it educates the conscience:

"Change yourself, not slyly, but sincerely,
Realize the needs of the enemy,
Your repentance is the keys to peace,
Buy relations and CALM inexpensively."
