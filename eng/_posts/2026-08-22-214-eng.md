Constancy brings
Healthy fruit.
It is hard for the frayed one to concentrate —
The treacherous strain of emotions lets him down,
He asks for rest,
Or there is not enough time,
For he trembles and awaits a near misfortune.

You and the circumstances. A lack of harmony.
How to achieve it?
Right laws, not ignored,
Honored from childhood
And masoned in old age
Gather and teach how to give.

What is stretched too far
Tears,
What is crumpled into a heap
Gets tangled.
What is well tended
Answers with good —
By rewards and thanks
It is inspired.
