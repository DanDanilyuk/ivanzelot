I am not Buddha and not Christ,
Do not call the paramedics.
My verbal diarrhea –
More from enthusiasms than from nightmares.

Neither depression nor euphoria
Has taken hold of consciousness.
Hope calls toward the goal,
So they would not leap, but quietly rejoice.

So I might delight in you,
And some of you — in me,
With healthy words
At peace with love.
