I've acquired. I have.
Fate gifted it.
But I wait for more,
That's not enough for me.

It comes. I take. That's how it's amassed.
I open the drawer,
And there it's packed...
They offer advice:

What I have
Is meant to help.
But it may also hinder,
Because I desire too much.
