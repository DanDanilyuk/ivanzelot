Secrets of being, encoded in the Word,
Go on living in thoughts and in sounds,
Flying off, they return again,
To compose themselves in knowledge and sciences.

They scatter in quarrels and bind together,
Lead to wars and rebuildings,
Divide into clans and groups,
Without tearing away from the foundations.

Let us argue, so as to part
To the ends of the earth and beyond the oceans,
So that later, coming together, more tightly weave
Sensations and experience in words,
So the code may fix itself more firmly.
