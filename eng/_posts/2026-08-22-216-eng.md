A cart stood there,
Bothering no one,
Until a "specialist" turned up —
And began to drag it.

What for? He itched
To show initiative,
An empty occupation, but that's his affair,
If it makes him happy.

"Only I am toiling," he says,
"And the others are idling?
Unfair. I'll go lie down,
Let those lazybones work."
