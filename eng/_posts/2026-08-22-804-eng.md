And let's add God up.
To the one the people loved,
We'll add Jehovah, that is the Living One,
And the one who created the Universe.

The God who led Abraham,
With the God of Isa of the Christians,
With Buddha, the God of nirvana
And Allah of the Muslim brothers,
With the gods of storm and swamp,
Of seas, stars and luminaries —

And we shall obtain Sabaoth,
The Essence of Space and Forces.
