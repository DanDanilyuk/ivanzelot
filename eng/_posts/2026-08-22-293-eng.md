I would hurry to help,
But I have knots enough of my own.
Of my own anxiety
That is all I know.

"You,—" they say, "—have it good,
It hurts us, not you.
Grief does not touch you,
And we are in shit."

So it turns out suddenly:
Those with a cold and a runny nose
Are served with ease
By those sick with cancer.
