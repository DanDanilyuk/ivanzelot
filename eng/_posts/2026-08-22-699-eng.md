To dissolve in God—to give oneself
In the aromas of the lived and the intended.
To regard one's present as His,
The elusive speck —
an overflowing of the eternal.

I came and I will go —
They did not know
And will not know.
From nowhere into fullness —
So that there they would receive,
Here they will drive out.

Appearance into the world —
Boundlessness of plenty.
Into endless flight
We will transform the period.
