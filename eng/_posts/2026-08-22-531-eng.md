A powerful order with many zeros
The client set before me.
I have no idea where to begin.
My capacities are quite small.

Competitors all around slip in bribes,
The tender seems already knocked out from under me,
They set traps on every side,
Offering a re-election.

The planet trembles with fear,
Tears off the scab of leprosy.
The Lord promises He will heal it.
I am who I am,
David of small stature.
