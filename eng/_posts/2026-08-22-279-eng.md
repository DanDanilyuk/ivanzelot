Jealousy in dreams
And trust when awake —
What further proof of love is needed?
Flirt and banter in words
Add a new strength of love
Without batteries, on the go,
Without oaths and virtuous pledges.

You simply breathe happiness,
Taking it as natural,
In the mornings you write poems down
About the Lord's blessing.

Fate is not your hopping through the bushes,
Not the wise thinker's success,
Not your parents' striving,
It comes from the Protector.
