In your heart's pocket
Your code,
Your key to understanding the world.
The overcoming of obstacles
And daily challenges
Grant you an education.

Like a flashlight,
It serves you best of all.
Let it not sadden you nor darken
The mockery of your being lost.

Everyone has their own flashlight
And their own unique path,
In the labyrinths their own crevices,
And yours —
In your heart and in your hands.
