The wisdom of chess — boundlessness of variants.
As in life —
Go and figure it out.
In their squares, treasures of talents.
Moves are simple,
But learn and learn.

Only 6 pieces,
6 statuses of life.
6 days the Lord
Created our world.
Ideas of storms
Are at work here too:
Enter into God
In the format of a game.

The law of life
Is laid into chess.
It is encoded,
Figure it out yourself.
Its fields —
Bliss and horrors,
Here you are either bound,
Or rejoice.
