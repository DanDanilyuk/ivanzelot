A sinner went to war for the truth,
A saint calls to love evil,
The church forces one to avoid it,
But Holy Scripture has not changed.

Cunning Levites sold themselves to mammon,
They gather what is alien and shear the money,
Sheep in the folds mixed with wolves,
They read prayers
And sing psalms,
The sightless lead the blind to the pit.

Life is an exam, a thorny road.
Cassocks and vestments — only a masquerade,
Only a conscience without poisonous advice will understand:
Whoever accepted Satan, that one renounces God.
