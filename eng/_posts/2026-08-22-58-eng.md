Saint Nicholas has returned.
Enough of Ded Moroz.
Remember the sovok's outrage, —
The fathers' suffering asks it.

The commie thought
He would break the faith.
Of Christmas only a shadow —
But he will not suffocate it

For some, Santa Claus,
For some, Père Noël.
What did we need
The godless "Metel'" for?
