Thunderstorms —
A condition for rainbows.
Tears
Carry an armful of happiness.

How to look around
To notice the positive?
Not to err in conclusions,
But to gather the blessings?

The sad I will always find,
No one even looks for it.
But whether I take it well —
They will ask at the Judgment.
