The ape learned to leap through the trees,
The human —
To lie, to kill and to steal,
The machine —
To control the planet:
Whom to lift up,
And whom to let fall low.

Progress steps forward,
Evolution is relentless,
Bayraktar and Shahed flew apart,
As consequence and cause.

If you wish, deepen the study of Truth,
If you wish, rejoice, or fall into depression,
But accept for the world this definition:
They are carrying us to an address.
