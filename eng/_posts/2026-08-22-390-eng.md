How do you listen?
For purity of speech?
Intonation?
The expressiveness of the word?
Someone's lecture?

Hearing is a sack
From which to sort,
Which thing is nourishing,
And what to throw away.

The valuable — into the treasury,
Trash — onto the garden.
Knowledge — onto the shelf
Of advice for rewards.
