Now — this is eternity itself,
And the given minute — its fragment.
Of Being, His Majesty,
Every moment proclaims.

It will not lose the past
And will not miss the future
The so-called present.
Though you burst.

Affirms on the fly
Its missions
What is painted on sand
Or written on water.
