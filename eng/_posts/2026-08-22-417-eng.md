Meeting yet another problem,
I take offense at fate.
Stumbling on a scheme of the solution,
I take pride in myself.

As foes and enemies
The irritants appear —
I want to avoid them.
Without agreeing to be their accomplices
It's impossible to grow.

The last challenge — death.
I SO want to live!
It follows, until I die,
I must befriend death.
