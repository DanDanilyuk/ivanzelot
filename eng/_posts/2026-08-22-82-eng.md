Do not fear a different thinking,
Allow yourself to argue.
From the beginnings of the calendar
Never will an end be found.

We taste the flavor of eternity.
Since there is no end,
Inflate, though it will deflate,
Not for yourself, for the glory of the Creator.

Not in vain are our souls' searches
In the spiritual gyms of words
And the refusal to make concessions
To the proposals of swindlers.
