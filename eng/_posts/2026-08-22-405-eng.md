In a world of wonders it grows ever harder to amaze,
Tsunamis of news and sensations approach.
Maybe simply acknowledge the Demiurge as better?
Let him LIFT us above the waves — and they won't sweep us away.

But no, we are interested in the very center of the hurricane,
The planet's core and the bounds of bounds.
The ocean's depths aren't enough for us
Nor the number of stars in telescopes either.

Clothe yourself in patience,
You are only God's likeness.
To remain a creation —
Is your nature.
A droplet absorbs and reflects
Everything around —
The Result and the Cause
Will look after it.
