The West builds,
The East destroys.
For example, Kyiv founds Moscow.
Surrounds the Kremlin with walls,
And then begs mercy from it,
From it brings upon itself
Disaster with its own hands.

The predator has lain in ambush,
The victim grew rich — it is time.
Glad of toilets and refrigerators —
Moscow will drink its fill of blood.

You multiply estates,
You BREED children.
You think you are doing good,
And at the end you find
That you yourself are bringing —
To yourself and to others —
Suffering and evil.
