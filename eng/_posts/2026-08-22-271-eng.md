What is packed with manure
Will yield well.
The humbled will be glorified.
Whoever harms today
Will break tomorrow.

The cunning will outwit himself,
It will flow to the simple.
It will fly from the stingy,
The destitute will find it.

How not to believe in karma?
In the circulation of things?
It is well conceived
Regarding all ideas.
