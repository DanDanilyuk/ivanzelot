I sought the word of the day outside,
But it was born INSIDE.
Inexpressible feelings were explained by it,
Better than yesterday, they are understood now.

I have grasped more deeply —
Tomorrow I will save,
I have LOOKED more widely —
I will walk the path faster,
I will break through obstacles.

I am sent into this world
Not to idle,
But to continue the good,
To show
That I am trying
Not to destroy,
But to multiply what is God's.
