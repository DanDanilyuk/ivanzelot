We draw little huts,
We put on vyshyvankas,
We pine for the village,
We lie down in the rye.

We love Ukraine,
And her kalyna,
Among ourselves we brawl
Even unto death.

Ready to perish
Again and again,
To soar to the stars,
Only not for the language.
