Someone got lucky —
Paints the leader.
Hopes —
They will pour out gold.
Meanwhile life
May strike with a hammer.

To grovel in order to swagger —
A thankless craft.
Wait for the losses,
For they will swallow you with relish.

Write for yourself,
Shine for yourself.
When the need comes —
They will come for the fruit.
