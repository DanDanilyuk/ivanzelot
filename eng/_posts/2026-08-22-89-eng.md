The altruist loves —
He longs to serve.
And so he comes to know
The pleasure of living.

The sadist, the masochist
Demands his own.
The egoist loves —
Evil cannot be escaped.

To enslave others —
Is slavery for oneself
(More responsibility).
Egoism is sacrilege.
