A time to sew and a time to rip,
To concentrate and to idle.
To work
And to go to sleep.
To flee from threats,
Or, opposite,
To stand before them.

Conditions will come to the circumstances,
If you recognize them.
You will mill a lot of chaff
Before you join all the intentions.

God will give the day,
God will give the food
And will prompt the needed plan.
And something fresh will come of it,
And will not undercut you
The ideological bedlam.
