Quantity or quality?
He fathered many children,
Amassed a lot of money,
Wrote a lot of articles.
Quantity.

Quality or quantity?
He taught the children,
Paid for the good,
Enriched society.
Quality.

He did not crawl, he flew,
Did not smolder,
But shone,
Did not watch, but ate,
Did not tremble,
And lived.
