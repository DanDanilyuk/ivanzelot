There is no Plan B.
More precisely, it will arise by itself
When the time comes.

Reserves will always be found
When you notice them,
Do not spend them at once,
Let them grow.

Do not worry,
Sleep peacefully.
C and D all the way to Ya will wait.
To reach the goal,
Find the strength
To carry what is at hand.
And the plans? They
Will find you themselves.
