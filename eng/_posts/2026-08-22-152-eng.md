I won't answer at once,
I'll wait it out,
I'll let the insult pass
And I won't suffer losses.

In a race — a mistake:
Excess tension,
A broken arrow,
Loss of a friend.

I won't hurry myself,
I'll pause him,
So that reliable words
May attain what is mine.
