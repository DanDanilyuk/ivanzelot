Scientists will meet in Berlin,
Olympians will gather in Tokyo,
Unknown yet who, and not today,—
They have not even held a colloquium,
Only voiced the idea,
And people already believed in it.

To flock together beyond the earth, —
Imagination is lacking, they take it for a lie,
The prophets have not testified enough.

The impossible becomes reality,
Taken as a given,
So we shall one day meet boundlessness too,
Having embraced the unattainable infinite.
