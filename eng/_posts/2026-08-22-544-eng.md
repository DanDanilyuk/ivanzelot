The wise will take wisdom from the unwise,
The fool will take the wisest for a fool.
Contest between them is barren and sad,
Until the wise pretends to be a pupil.

Temporarily, to avoid quarrels,
The spreading of ill fame,
Gathering instead the nectar
From uncomfortable circumstances.

For an invention, a higher idea
Is at the cutting edge of problems' halt,
Especially someone's nonsense
Helps one become an exemplar.
