At night I learned a great deal,
I will try to sleep in the day as well,
All the more, today is Sunday,
I sorted the chaotic thoughts,
Hope appeared clearly,
It was not without inspirations.

Zombies work without rest,
Meanings in a fog, mountains of duties.
Sometimes one needs to make a stop,
To hear what God is saying.

For fullness
Not much is needed.
Leave the earthly to the earth,
Concentrate on the soul —
Treasures
To gather for heaven.
