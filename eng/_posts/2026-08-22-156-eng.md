I call Him Allah,
God and Jehovah.
With love and fear
I attend upon Him.

Though the names are different
And they are countless,
We choose the native ones —
And in this is our weakness:

Who is the true one,
We argue
In contests of hearts.
Until we guess:
It is He —
Our Creator.
