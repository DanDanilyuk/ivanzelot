Despite the clouds the sun is above them,
Light and warmth over the obstacles,
Below a human character is formed,
A people obedient to truth, or a turbulent people.

Some will understand that twilight is a blessing,
Others will drive the darkness clean away.
But best is to find the balance —
Both sun and clouds bring benefit.

To water with rain,
To harvest in drought.
Not to fear change.
If only one knows
Where to expose oneself,
And where to hide.
