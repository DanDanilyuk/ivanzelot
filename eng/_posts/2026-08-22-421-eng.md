To appear happier than you really are —
A subconscious need to lift the spirits,
A mood nobody would take away,
With control of your own thought and voice.

To reign, not fall as a victim —
Positive emotions will help,
Yield where you must, stand stubborn somewhere,
Don't belittle your girlfriends, and please a boy.

Have you made the effort to show yourself
Clever, kind, and cheerful?
You won't wait long —
They'll call you queen of beauty.
