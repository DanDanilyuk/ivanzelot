Life will go on,
The sun will shine,
I will leave you,
But I will live with you.

You will bury the body,
At the funeral you will weep,
In parting you will remember,
In eternity you will see.

Thunderstorms will rumble out and flash away,
After incomplete old age
Temporary tears
Will become boundless joy.
