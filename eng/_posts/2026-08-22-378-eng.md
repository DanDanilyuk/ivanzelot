Judas informs on the teacher to the scribe —
How do you like such a picture?
A saint helps the robber,
A father betrays his son.

As if against the stronger
One need not resist.
To indulge the sinful one
Does the Creator require of you?

You will meekly commit violence
Upon those close to you and your own people —
And you will strictly judge yourself,
If not today, then in time.
