Wonderful!
But praise not me,
But the one who the Word
And the Word gives.
Spreads Him, sows Him,
With the Word cares for the Word.

The calling is given selectively —
At the treasury of spiritual riches
I too have been counted as Ali Baba,
But let us acknowledge the owner without sacrilege.

Did you like the verse, does it inspire you?
Has respect for the author been born?
Though the writer seeks it,
The inspiration is not from his source.
