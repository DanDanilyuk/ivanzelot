While they bomb Kyiv,
Be silent about politics.
«Let's not escalate,»
Not to traumatize the psyche.

But Moscow burns —
What inhumans you are.
«I hate the khokhol!» —
It's time to set for him
Traps and nets.

National interests —
Tension in the families.
Brothers and sisters
Forget about humanity.
