The last?…
Not at all.
In truth the first.
In truth
That copper coin
Will outdo them all.

Just arrived,
And at once it buys.
Gave itself and again —
It trades-and-turns.

At the bottom of the purse not so fertile,
The last receives first.
Successful is the restless one,
Clear the way for the stubborn!
